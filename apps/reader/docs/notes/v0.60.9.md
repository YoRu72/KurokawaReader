### Fixes
- **A hold undone by letting go**: on a phone, holding a book (or a chapter, to start picking) sometimes selected it and then unselected it as your finger lifted. The hold now counts once, and the tap that comes with letting go is ignored.

### Keyboard (computer)
- **Library**:

  | Key | Does |
  |---|---|
  | Ctrl+A | Select every book shown |
  | Ctrl+F | Search |
  | Delete | Remove the selected books (asks first) |
  | Enter | Open the one selected |
  | Esc | Leave the selection |
