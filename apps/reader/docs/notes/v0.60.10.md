### Fixes
- **Updates on a weak phone link stopped at the very end** («The connection dropped — trying again (3 of 3)»): a connection that kept dropping used up its retries even though each try brought more of the file. A retry that brings bytes no longer counts as a failure, and the update keeps going from where it stopped.
- **The library's selection bar ran off the edge** of medium-wide windows, hiding Convert and the actions after it. Whatever doesn't fit now goes behind **More**.

### Add to the library while browsing (as in Mihon)
- **Hold a cover in a source's list** and a series or web novel goes **straight into your library**, nothing downloaded: its volumes or chapters stay on the source until you read them. Your tags are asked as before, and the cover is marked «In library» at once.
- **Possible duplicates**: if your library already has something with a similar name, you're shown it first. You can open it, or add this one anyway.
- Holding something that's already in your library (or a single file) still opens the full list of choices.

### PDF → Word
- Computers with many cores now read a long PDF in up to six lanes at once (four before). Phones are unchanged: eight cores give three lanes.
