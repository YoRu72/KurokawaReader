### Fixes — PDF → Word, page numbers and running heads
- **Page numbers are real page numbers.** They were plain text; each is now a Word page-number field in the header or footer, at the place and on the side the page has it, in the page's own digits (١٢٣, 123, roman), with decorations such as «- 16 -» kept. Each page states its own number, so Word shows the number printed in the PDF, also where a book restarts its numbering.
- **Page numbers and running heads no longer fall into the text.** On pages where the text starts close to the head, or the head is long, the number and head were written as the first paragraph of the page (33 pages of one tested 1,061-page book, 57 of another book).
- **Headings and first lines no longer taken for a running head.** A line standing apart at the top of a page that is not repeated on nearby pages is put back into the text.
- **Pages cut into boxes only because of their page number** are written as flowing text like the rest of the book.
- A page whose margins were measured larger than the page itself is corrected.
- One page = one section in every tested book.

### Known, not yet fixed
- A page number that cannot be confirmed against a nearby page stays plain text.
- Brackets around a number in Arabic text can still come out mirrored.
