### Fixes
- **«App blocked to protect your device» (Google Play Protect)**: Play Protect now blocks any app installed from a file that declares an accessibility service, and the screen translator's text reader was one. The ordinary APKs (`*-arm64-v8a.apk`, `*-armeabi-v7a.apk`, `*-universal.apk`) no longer declare it, so they install as before; in them the screen translator reads the screen as a picture (OCR). Whoever wants the exact-text reader — auto translation as you scroll — installs the separate `*-a11y.apk` of the same release: it installs over the ordinary one, keeps everything, and Play Protect may ask you to allow it once. The updater keeps whichever variant you have; the screen translator's page says which you are on and links the other.
- **Add-on packs that failed with «HTTP 504»** (mpv, ffmpeg, Python): GitHub answers a 504 or cuts a transfer now and then, and one such answer failed the whole download. The fetch is now tried up to six times with a growing pause, taking up where it stopped rather than starting over — on the phone and on the desktop alike.
- **The download card that stayed at the bottom of every page**: a pack or add-on card that was waiting for «Get it», or had failed and offered Retry, leaves with the page it was asked on; a running download still keeps its card wherever you go.
