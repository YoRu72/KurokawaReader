### Fixes — the phone's Back, and being sent out of a book
- **The phone's Back swipe works again in a book.** Edge protection came switched on in 0.61.0 and took over a wide strip of both screen edges, so a Back swipe turned the chapter. It is now **off by default** (this update turns it off once for everyone; switch it on again in Settings if you want it).
- **A page-turn swipe no longer sends you out of the book.** When one swipe both turned the chapter and counted as the phone's Back, the reader obeyed the Back. A Back that comes as part of a page-turn swipe is now ignored; a deliberate Back leaves at once, to where the book was opened from.
- **Edge protection, when you switch it on, is lighter**: only the middle of each edge, and only while the bars are hidden. Back always works from the top and bottom of the edge, from the button, and whenever the bars show.

### Fixes — EPUB
- **Words stuck together** («name’sRudeus»): a space the book kept in a piece of its own was dropped. It is kept now.
- **Chapter titles are centred**, and so are the lines the book itself centres: point-of-view names under a title, scene breaks, dedications.
- **The book's own bold and sizes are shown**, relative to your reading size. Titles the book styles itself come out bold at its own modest size.
- **The first letter of a chapter** is bold at text size, on its line. The book's large letter is one choice away.
- **Empty lines between a title and the text** no longer pile into a large gap.

### Fixes — the app closing by itself while reading
- **Books you had closed stayed in memory.** Every book opened kept its whole text and pictures after you left it, until the phone ran short and ended the app. A closed book is now let go of. (This had been so since the first version.)
- **Pictures, once shown, were never released.** Reading a book with many pictures took more and more memory, without end. Only the pictures near the screen are kept now; the others come back as you reach them, and the text does not move.
- **Android: when the page's engine fails, the reader restarts cleanly** and returns to your book. This now also covers the app's hidden pages (the in-app browser's, printing), any of which could close the whole app while you read; and it gives up after three failures in three minutes in place of restarting for ever.

### Changed
- **Hyphenation is on by default.** This update turns it on once for everyone; turn it off in the reading options and it stays off. Titles are never hyphenated.

### New
- **Chapter titles**: Centred / At the start / As the book says.
- **Hide the word «Chapter»** in titles and in the bars (off by default).
- **The book's own sizes and bold** (on by default).
- **First letter of a chapter**: Bold, text size / As the book sets it / Plain.
- **The Performance page says why a session ended** (Settings → About → Performance): the page's engine ended by the system, a crash, the app not responding, or ended for memory — and now also each Back that was ignored as part of a page-turn swipe. Times, counts and kinds only; nothing is sent anywhere.

### Known
- Hyphenation depends on the system's dictionaries; on Windows and Linux it may not take effect yet.
- A book whose gap between two words exists only in its styling, not as a real space, can still show them joined.
