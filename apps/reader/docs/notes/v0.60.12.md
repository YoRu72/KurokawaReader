### Fixes
- **PDF search: tapping a result now always goes to its page.** In a long book, a result tapped while the search was still reading pages did nothing, and the reader stayed where it was.
- **PDF search no longer starts over.** Closing the search and opening it again shows the same search where it stands (still running or finished), instead of reading the book from the first page again.
- **PDF zoom buttons: every tap counts.** Tapping zoom again before the previous zoom finished could lose the tap, jump to a different page, or leave the page stretched. The point at the centre of the screen now stays put.
- The word found is highlighted on far pages of long books too (the reader waits for the page's text for up to ten seconds, not three).
- The result count shown during a search keeps up with the list.
