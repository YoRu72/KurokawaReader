### Fixes
- **«Save to device» no longer lists the same file several times.** Sharing a file again replaces the old entry, and a new share clears what was left from earlier ones.
- **Bluetooth devices in use no longer read as «not connected»** on recent Android. The state now comes from several sources together (audio, calls, input devices, low-energy links) instead of one call that Android may refuse.
- **Other apps now get Tools' own file browser** when they ask for a file, for everyone. Before, it stayed on Android's screen if another app already used Tools' storage places. Those apps' saved links may need choosing again, or switch «Storage places» back on in the picker setting.
- **A switch's explanation sits on its own line** everywhere; it was glued to the label in the download's «Get a new link by itself» card.
- The last plain checkboxes are switches; small buttons, chips and row icons are larger under the finger on the phone; buttons in one row have equal widths; chips no longer touch; tints taken from covers are never warm.

### New
- **«Save to device»: choose and remove.** Tick the files to save, remove one with its × (with Undo), and «Save here» says how many it will save.
- **Bluetooth cards update by themselves** when a device connects, leaves, or reports a new level, and when Bluetooth is turned off or on.
- **Device details:** tap a card for its names, Bluetooth type, class, pairing, what it is used for now, what it offers, the audio codec where Android tells it, where the level comes from, and when it was first and last seen. Nothing is connected to or written to.
- **The picker opens as a list** with item counts and dates, a close button and search.
- The Bluetooth page redraws only the cards that changed and does nothing while hidden.

### Not checked on real devices
- The new Bluetooth sources, the codec and earbud levels on Android 16.
- Whether other apps' saved links work again after «Storage places» is switched back on.
