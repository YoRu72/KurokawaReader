### Fixes
- **In-place unpacking is hardened.** The known limit of 0.20.5 is closed: job files are validated before use, restore never overwrites a file that is not the job's own, a job found beside an archive is finished only when this app started it, and an archive that was replaced beside a stopped job opens as itself. A blocked job can be forgotten (its records only).
- **The new computer build now has the unpacking shelf, undo and byte-for-byte restore** that only the previous build had.
- **Making a zip never replaces an existing file unasked.**
- **Outside players get subtitles again**, and sign-in headers are never placed on a command line.
- **A stream is released only when nothing will play it**, and the «watched?» question no longer holds it open.
- **The player's local addresses are signed per run**, and a page's cookies go only to the site they came from.
- **Arabic shows the digits 0-9** unless you choose otherwise; the Digits setting had no effect before.
- **Arabic wording and right-to-left layout** corrected across the player settings, archive questions and toasts: paths and names no longer flip, buttons in a row share a width.

### New: device battery
- **A page for the batteries of your devices:** game controllers, mice, keyboards, headsets, microphones, earbuds with each bud and the case, and a phone through Phone Link. 75 device families, including those of BatteryHub 1.1.0.
- **On the taskbar, as in the original tool:** a tray icon per device with its level drawn in it (or one icon with the lowest level, or the app's icon with a menu), as a number or a small battery; red when low, blue while charging, a check when full, dimmed when asleep. Experimental and off by default: a strip on the taskbar itself beside the notification area.
- **Low and full notices** with a mark per device (5% to 40%), sound on or off, and quiet hours. Checking every 30 seconds to every hour.
- **History and time left:** each device's level is recorded and shown as a chart of the last day, week or month; a card says «about N hours left» or «full in about N minutes» when the readings support it. Kept 7, 30 or 90 days, on this computer only.
- **Your devices your way:** rename, choose one of our drawn pictures, hide, move up or down, join two entries of one device.
- **Runs in the tray**, and can start hidden with the system (off until you switch it on).
- **Connect and disconnect Bluetooth devices from Tools**, and find and pair new ones. Pairing is confirmed in the system's own window; the app never enters or keeps a PIN.
- **Teach Tools a device it does not know:** «Teach me this device» watches what a connected device sends, you mark «full», «about half», «low», and Tools proposes where the level is. The result is a small definition file you can export and import. A definition only listens: it cannot send anything to a device, and keyboards, mice, media keys and pen tablets are never listened to.
- **Report a device:** a text for developers listing the devices the app sees, with no serial numbers, addresses, names or folders in it. You read and edit it before copying; the app sends nothing.
- **Command line:** `--battery-json` prints the devices once as JSON and exits; `--battery-help` shows the help.
- **Linux:** reads the batteries the kernel knows, Bluetooth levels, and the makers' readers through read-only device nodes. If a device is closed to you, the page shows a rule for the reader's own interface of the listed devices and the commands to install it; Tools never runs sudo.
- **macOS:** shows the levels macOS itself reports (Apple keyboards, mice and trackpads, Bluetooth devices, earbuds with left, right and case). The makers' readers are not on macOS yet.
- **Android:** the batteries of your paired Bluetooth devices as Android reports them, with optional low notices while the app is open. It asks the «Nearby devices» permission only when you tap Allow, and never scans, pairs or asks for your location.
- **Safe by design:** the app only reads. Devices that must be asked before they answer are off until you allow their family, and the app can send only a fixed list of known questions.
- **Not yet tried on real hardware.** Every reader is ported and tested against made-up examples only. A device may be missing or show a wrong level; please tell us which.
- Not taken from the original: its automatic updater (Tools updates through its own releases) and its makers' brand colours.

### New: system on the taskbar (Windows, experimental)
- **CPU load and temperature, memory, graphics load, temperature and memory, and the system drive's temperature**, beside your devices on the taskbar strip or as tray icons with the number drawn. Off by default; you choose the values and their order, °C or °F, reading every 1, 2 or 5 seconds, and what a click does.
- **No driver and no administrator rights:** a value Windows does not give an ordinary program is not shown, and the card says so. On many computers that includes the CPU temperature.
- Temperatures turn rose when warm and red when hot, at marks you can change. Reading pauses while the screen is off or the computer is locked.
- Inspired by TraySense by DMTWEAKS; no code taken.

### New: fast file search (computer)
- **«Everywhere in my folders» in Files** (Ctrl+Shift+F): choose folders once, then find files by name as you type. The index follows changes, is kept in one checked file, and nothing leaves the computer.
- It can pause while the computer is on battery.

### New: watching
- **Choose how each kind of video plays:** the app's player, a player on this computer, or ask each time. The position comes back into your watched state.
- **Clear notices** for a file with no sound or a picture that cannot be decoded. Protected streams are refused up front.

### New: animated backgrounds
- **A scene engine with fourteen scenes** (snowfall, rain, aurora over a lake and more) that react to the pointer and keep text readable.
- **Mix your own:** a ground, a scene and up to two overlays with amount, size and wind; Snowy and Rainy presets; saved mixes.
- **Your own pictures and videos as backgrounds**, with fit, veil, blur and colours matched to the picture.
- **A browser for a Wallpaper Engine library** on this computer: its video wallpapers can be copied in.

### New: themes
- **Twelve new themes** and a real editor: two accents, tint, glass, roundness, spacing, size, and colours per background. Themes are plain files you can export and import.
- Yellow and orange are not used: a colour in that range moves to the nearest one allowed, and text that would be too faint is corrected on save.
