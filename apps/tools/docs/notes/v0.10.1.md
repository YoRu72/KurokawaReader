### Fixes
- **No more false "the app closed" reports, and no more torrents paused for nothing.** When Android or the phone's power manager closes the app, an update replaces it, or it is swiped away from recent apps, Android records a signal (SIGINT, SIGTERM). The app took those for crashes. After two of them it reported a crash and kept the torrents paused at the next start. Now only real crashes count: a bad memory read, an abort, a bus or arithmetic fault, an illegal instruction, or the app not responding. Android closing the app to free memory is still noted, but no longer pauses anything.
