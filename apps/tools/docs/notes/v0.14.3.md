### Fixes
- **Files named `.bin` or with no extension** are now named for what they really are on PC/Mac too, once downloaded (an MP4, a PDF, an APK, an EPUB…), as they already were on Android.
- **Subtitles inside a video file** (a Matroska's SRT and ASS tracks) are now found and offered when the browser's player plays the file (needs the ffmpeg add-on). mpv already read them.
- **Subtitle translations are kept on the device**: a subtitle translated once comes back translated the next time, with no connection, and a translation stopped midway carries on where it stopped.
- Dead code removed across the app.

### New download dialog
Modelled on the best download managers:
- **The link is looked at before you download**: the file's size, the name the site gives it, and the extension read from the file's own bytes — so `file.bin` becomes `report.pdf` before it even starts.
- **Name and extension apart**, each free to change.
- **Free storage** shown, with a warning when the file won't fit.
- **Recent folders** to pick from with one tap.
- **A warning when a link gives a web page** (a sign-in, a check) instead of a file.
- **Referer page** field, for sites that need it.
- Per download: **Wi‑Fi only**, **retry if it fails**, **hidden file**, **start paused**.

### Player
- **Preferences laid out like REX Player**: Appearance (accent colour, seek bar style, shade under the controls, control size, title, chapter marks), Layout (the bar's tools, ordered), Gestures, Controls, Playback, Subtitles, Sound, Decoder, Picture — with a search box.
- **Pinch the subtitles to resize them**, **swipe them sideways** to jump to the next or previous line.
- **Save the current subtitle as SRT** (a translation, or a track taken out of the file).
- **One-tap switch** between the translation only, the translation with the original, and the original.

### Share over Wi‑Fi
- **Share finished files with another phone or computer**: a QR code and an address; the other device opens it in a browser and downloads. From a download's page, or all finished files from the Downloads menu. On Android and PC/Mac.
