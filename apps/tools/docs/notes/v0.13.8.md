### Fixes
- **Anime search finds more.** When the words as typed find nothing, the search tries again with them cleaned up, then shortened. It handles a release's file name (`[Group] Title - 05 [1080p].mkv`), «Season 2», «2nd season», «Part 2», «S2», an episode number, a year, and stray punctuation. The results say which words found them.

### New
- **Tags and filters for TV & Movies search.**
  - Narrow the results to series or films, to a lowest rating (★ 6+, 7+, 8+), and to one or more genres. The genres are shown as tags, most common first.
  - Put the results in order: best match, top rated, newest, oldest, or by title.
  - Genres and ratings are filled in from each title's page as the results come.
- **Smarter TV & Movies search.**
  - A release's file name (`Dune.2021.1080p.WEB-DL.x264`) is searched as its title, and titles of the year it names come first.
  - «Season 2», «S01E02» and «Part 1» are left out of the search.
  - When nothing is found, shorter forms of the words are tried.
  - A pasted IMDb link or id (`tt…`) opens its page straight away.
