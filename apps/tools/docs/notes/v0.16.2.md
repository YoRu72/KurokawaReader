### Fixes
- **Player — sound with a black picture (MKV and anything else mpv plays)**: when the player closed, mpv's video output was switched off with the picture's surface, and the next file was loaded without switching it back on. The first video after starting the app showed its picture; every one after it — the next episode, another file, the same file reopened — played its sound over black. The output is now switched back on whenever a new surface arrives.
- **Player — the ambient glow over mpv**: the glow fills the stage with a tinted layer that covered mpv's picture (which sits under the page); it is left out while mpv plays.

### Anime — a new Discover page
- **Discover** opens the Anime tab: a hero of what is trending now (its picture, format, genres, length, years, whether it is ongoing, its score and studio, a few lines of its story; it turns by itself and by a swipe), then rows that scroll sideways — **Continue watching** (how many minutes of an episode are left, «Next up» once the next one is out, «Airs in …» when it is not yet), **Trending today** ranked, **Airing now** marked «New episode», «New series» or «New season», **this season**, the best rated of all time and the popular films.
- **Studios**: MAPPA, ufotable, Kyoto Animation, Madhouse, Production I.G, Bones, Wit, A-1 Pictures, CloverWorks, Trigger and more, each over the picture of its best known show; a tap opens the **studio's page** — its works by popularity, score, newest or oldest, its own productions or with the ones it helped on.
- **Search by studio**: typing a studio's name in Search shows the matching studios above the shows; the studio on a show's page is a link to its page.
- **Genres, seasons and decades** as picture tiles: a tap opens the search on that genre, that season, or that decade (a new filter: from one year to another).
- On a computer, each row has arrows; with motion reduced, nothing moves by itself.

### TV & Movies — the same new Discover
- A hero of the popular series and films with their own wide pictures and title logos, **Continue watching** (minutes left of a film or an episode, the next episode once it is out), popular series and films ranked, this year's series and films, the best rated, animated films, and the **genres** as picture tiles that open **Browse** on them.
- The old Discover grid with its filters is now the **Browse** tab.
