## Fixed

- **PDF to Word: papers.** The abstract comes out as one inset, justified paragraph instead of oddly broken lines; a stacked fraction in a displayed equation stays on the equation's line; the left column ends where it ended on the page instead of sending its last line to the next column; references keep their order and hang like a list.
- **PDF to Word: books.** A drop cap is the first letter of its line again, a contents page keeps one entry per line with its page number, a block quote stays one paragraph, an ornament line («* * *») is no longer a numbered list, and running heads are not taken for text.
- **PDF to Word: Arabic.** A line of verse keeps its two halves, the right half first, and a Latin word inside an Arabic line stays in its place.
- **PDF to Word: slides, invoices and forms.** White text on a dark slide is readable in *Easy to edit*; bullets beside a picture are no longer pushed to the far side; an address set flush right is one line per line; a short word beside a number is no longer raised like a note mark; in *Exact look* a line at the edge of its frame is no longer cut off, and a page's number is no longer printed twice.
- **PDF to Word: affiliation marks and exponents** are no longer taken for footnotes.

## New: PDF to Word
- **The fonts, inside the file or in a folder.** A new choice under *Result*: *Inside the file* (as before) or *In a folder*. With *In a folder* the Word file carries no fonts, and the PDF's own fonts, cut down to the letters it uses, are written beside it as files you can install.
- **No font twice.** A PDF often carries the same font several times over; the folder holds each once, and a font that is already there from an earlier conversion is not written again.
- **Choose where it goes.** *Save to* lets you pick the folder for the document and, apart from it, the folder for the fonts. On the PC and on Android the files are written there directly; elsewhere you get one zip holding the document and its fonts. The finished card says what was saved and where.
- The converter is now measured on six more kinds of document (invoice, paper, Arabic text, slides, form, book), each made into PDF by two or three programs, beside the twelve it was measured on before.

## New: PC
- **One PC build.** The Windows, macOS and Linux builds are the new native ones only; the older builds are no longer made. A copy of the older build updates itself to the new one and takes your library with it.
