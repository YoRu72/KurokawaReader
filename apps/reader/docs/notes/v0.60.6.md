### Computer
- **The library made for a computer**: a page title instead of a phone's large heading, and one row of tools sized for a mouse — the search and the order first, the other tools as small buttons with their names on hover. The covers sit in a wider, tidier grid, and «Import books» is no longer repeated on the page (it's at the top of the sidebar).
- **Right-click a book** for its menu at the pointer — open, favourite, to read, mark as read, tags, details, review, convert, move, share, delete — instead of it being chosen. Ctrl+click still chooses several.
- **Browse is in the sidebar**, and stays lit in a catalog; a book's page keeps Library lit.
- Choice buttons drop down under themselves; the other pages' titles are larger; Settings' and Notes' titles are a computer's size.

### Icons
- **The app's own icon, of your choosing** (Settings → Appearance): pick a picture and crop it — move, zoom, turn, round, rounded or square, a fill colour. On a computer the window, the taskbar, the Dock and the shortcuts show it; on Android a home-screen shortcut with it.
