### Fixes
- **Japanese (and any left-to-right) text no longer turns right-to-left after translating to Arabic.** When a translation was taken back, a paragraph lost the direction the book gave it and took its chapter's, so lines were right-aligned with their full stops at the start. Each paragraph's own direction is now kept and put back exactly, including in «show the original too».
- **The translator's bar fits the screen.** Its buttons no longer run off the edge. The status shortens instead, and the less used actions are in one ⋮ menu: Translate ahead, Find names for the glossary, Clear the translations, Customise.

### New
- **Translate the whole book in the background.** A range of chapters, or the whole book, now runs as a job. You can keep reading and scrolling, leave the book, or put the app in the background. On Android, the progress shows in a notification («Paragraphs: 120 / 800»), and a notification says when it is done. The app also shows its progress in the jobs chip, where you can stop it.
