### Fixes — translator, glossary, sources
- **Glossary no longer changes the inside of other words.** A name such as «Lin» was replaced inside «Linda»; with both «Lin» and «Lin Feng» listed, the shorter one could win; and a replacement could itself be replaced again. The longest name now wins, whole words only for Latin-style scripts (Arabic, Japanese, Chinese and Korean terms match as before), and each place is changed once.
- **AI keys and app-wide settings are no longer saved into one book.** Typed while a book with its own settings was open, they applied to that book only. They are app-wide now, and ones already saved into a book are moved back. "Reset defaults" in the reading options resets the reading options only — it used to be able to wipe the AI key and sync settings.
- **Very short chapters download.** A chapter of a few words («The End.», «تمت.») was taken for an empty page, so "download all" never finished.
- **Updates from a beta** to the final version of the same number are now offered.
- **Moving a novel to another source** keeps chapters of different volumes apart when their numbers restart in each volume.
- **Text rules**: `\p{L}` and other Unicode classes now work; a rule of a shape that can freeze the reader is refused when saved, with a message.
- **On the computer, every question the app asks uses its own dialog**: PDF passwords in PDF tools and the converter, folder and shelf names, theme name. These did nothing before.
- **Translations brought back from a backup** are no longer overwritten by the open translator.
