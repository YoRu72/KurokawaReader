### New
- **Custom APIs: any AI service for the translator.** Paste an API key and the app works out which service it is for.
  - **Recognised by the key:** Grok (xAI), Groq, OpenRouter, Anthropic, Gemini, Cerebras, Perplexity, Fireworks, Hugging Face, NVIDIA, GitHub Models, Together and Chutes.
  - **Picked from a short list when several services share the key's form:** OpenAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot (Kimi), Zhipu (GLM), Mistral, DeepInfra and OpenCode. A key is never sent to a service it might not be for.
  - **Any other service, or a server of your own** (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM): add its address too, and the app finds how to speak to it (OpenAI-, Anthropic- or Gemini-style).
  - **Its models are listed with what the service says of them:** free ones first and marked, the price per million tokens (in / out), how many tokens each takes in and gives out, and any the service marks unavailable. Filter them, pick one, then **try it** (it answers, or you are told why not: wrong key, needs credit, limited for now) and keep it.
  - **Several can be kept** and switched between. Each shows how much it was used today (requests, tokens in and out) and the cost, when the service gives prices.
  - Extra headers can be added, for proxies and gateways.
- **OpenCode Zen** as a provider: its key, its models from Browse models, and requests through the app's own connection.
