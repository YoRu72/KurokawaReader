### Fixes — the reading place
- **A book reopened at its saved place stays there.** In a long text book, and in an EPUB still being prepared, the text could slide away a moment after opening — some seventy paragraphs in a long book — and the place it slid to could then be saved as yours. Most seen on Mac and iPhone; on Android in long text books. The reader now holds your paragraph itself through everything that changes the page after it opens (sections taking their real height, fonts arriving, pictures loading, the window or zoom changing), until you scroll.
- **A place is never saved from text that only moved by itself.** Only your own scroll, a page turn or a jump changes the saved place.
- An EPUB could save its place a chapter off and reopen several chapters back; the paragraph on screen is now read correctly.
- Your own scrolling is never fought: search results, read-aloud, auto-scroll, page keys and taps, chapter turns and "back to where I was" all keep working as before.
