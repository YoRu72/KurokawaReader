### Fixes
- **The icon cropper's zoom icon filled the whole window**, pushing the picture out of sight. It is a small icon beside the slider again, and «back to the start» has its own icon instead of the same one as «turn».
- **A computer's first start fetched the add-ons but not their parts**: ffmpeg now comes with the Player (and the Video add-on) right away, the usual card showing it coming.

### Computer
- **The window's bar is the app's own**: the system's plain bar is gone; in its place a strip in the theme's colours with the app's icon, its name and the place you're in («Kurokawa Tools — Downloads»). Windows' minimise, maximise and close buttons stay, drawn in the same colours, and follow a light or dark theme; on a Mac its three lights sit inset. The bar hides in full screen, and you can still drag the window by it and double-click to maximise it.

### Icons
- **The icon of your choosing inside the app too**: the logo in the bar and the window's little icon change with it, not only the desktop shortcuts and the taskbar, and it stays after a restart. On a phone the app's logos change as well; Android lets an app add a home-screen shortcut with your icon but not replace its own launcher icon.
