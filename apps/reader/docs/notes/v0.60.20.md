### Fixes — PDF → Word, lines, verses and paragraphs
- **Poetry.** Numbered verses, and verses whose halves are not both stretched, came out as one justified paragraph with the two halves pushed together. Each verse is now a row of its own with its two halves set apart, as on the page. In one tested book of verse this went from 7 verses recognised to 1,010.
- **Verses out of order.** On some pages all the left halves came out together, apart from the right halves. They now read verse by verse.
- **Separate paragraphs joined into one**, and **numbered items joined together** (with words cut out of each item into a column of their own): each now starts its own paragraph.
- **Pages wrongly read as two columns.** A one-column page whose lines happened to leave a gap at one place was cut in two; this also made the Word file hold more sections than the PDF has pages. The tested books now have one section per page.
- **«هـ» of hijri years** lost its tatweel in justified paragraphs («١٧٥ه»); it is kept.

### Known, not yet fixed
- A paragraph break marked only by a slightly wider gap (no indent, no number) can still be missed.
- On one designed page, the short last line of a quote box became a paragraph of its own.
