### Fixes (downloads)
- **Slow downloads on an unsteady connection.** When the connection dropped for a moment, the download lost one of its parallel connections for good, and the app remembered the lower number for that site, so later downloads from it stayed slow. A drop is now just retried. Only a site that truly refuses extra connections lowers the count, and sites already marked down recover within a few downloads.
- **A speed limit under 16 KB/s no longer freezes a download.** It sat at 0 B/s and pausing didn't help.
- **Two downloads with the same file name no longer write into one file.** The second is saved as "name (2)". This could happen with a Google Drive folder holding two files of one name, a double tap on Download, or the same link twice in a batch.
- **"Wi-Fi only" is respected when you add or resume a download on mobile data.** It waits, shown as "Waiting for Wi-Fi", and starts by itself on Wi-Fi. The same goes for the low-battery pause.
- **"Download now"** (new) on a waiting download starts it anyway, when you want that one on mobile data.
- **The schedule no longer resumes downloads you paused yourself**, and no longer pauses ones you started by hand.
- **Setting a download's start time no longer erases its stop time**, or the other way round.
- **A link with a stray "%" in it can be added.** In a batch, one bad link no longer stops the rest: the others are added and the failed ones stay in the box.
- **Downloads of unknown size** show a working bar, not "5 MB of -1 B".
- Pause followed at once by Resume no longer leaves the download paused.
- A MEGA or Google Drive download whose link ran out is given a fresh link again later, not only once.
- A paused MEGA download resumes where it stopped in a case where it started over.
- Watching a remote aria2 no longer piles up background requests.

Note: "Resume all" no longer starts Wi-Fi-only downloads on mobile data. Use "Download now" on the ones you want.

### Video downloads: choose what you get
- **Video**: pick the quality (only the ones the video really has, with their approximate size) and the file type: MP4 (plays everywhere), MKV or WebM. Under Advanced: prefer 60 fps, and smaller files (AV1/VP9) or compatible ones (H.264).
- **Audio only**: MP3 (320, 256, 192, 128 kbps or best variable), M4A or Opus taken as they are (no conversion, the fastest, and no ffmpeg needed), FLAC or WAV, with the cover picture and the title/artist tags.
- **Extras**: subtitles (your languages, automatic ones marked, inside the file or beside it), chapters (kept, or the video split by them), SponsorBlock (remove or mark segments), a thumbnail file, and the file name (Title; Uploader - Title; Playlist/NN - Title).
- **Playlists**: this video only, the whole list, or a range such as `1-5, 8`.
- Your last choice is remembered. Save your own named presets ("Music MP3 320", "Phone 720p"), and choose between "Always ask" and one-tap adds with your default. The same default applies wherever a video is added.
- ffmpeg is asked for only when your choice needs it, no longer every time a video page is read.

### Video downloads: faster
- A video's details are read once, not again when you press Download.
- Streams that come in pieces are downloaded several pieces at a time (Settings → "Parallel pieces": Auto, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16).
- No conversion unless your choice needs one: MP4 picks streams that only need joining.
- Progress shows what is happening: downloading video, downloading audio, joining, converting to MP3, adding the cover.
