### Fixes
- **Watch while it downloads, on computers too.** On Windows, macOS and Linux, «Watch» on an episode or a film said it «needs the app on a phone or computer». It now plays at once: the app serves the file to the player as its pieces come in, and asks the download to fetch them in order, from the start. If you skip ahead, playback waits for that part to arrive. A download you are watching fetches its first and last pieces first, which the player reads before anything else.

### New
- **Faster updates on computers.** An update is now downloaded over eight connections at once, like the phone's downloader. On Windows, the installed app is updated quietly, with no installer wizard to click through, and opens again when it is done. The portable app still opens its installer, as before.
- **Fonts for styled subtitles.**
  - On a computer, the fonts installed on it are now found by themselves and used for styled (ASS) subtitles: Windows', macOS' and Linux's font folders, your own included. Only each font's names are read, so a thousand fonts take a moment. You can turn this off in *Settings → Player*.
  - On Android, the fonts folder can now be picked with Android's folder chooser, instead of typing its path.
