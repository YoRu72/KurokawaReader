### Android: more of the app's work done by its Rust part, for you to try
Nothing in this release changes how the app behaves until you choose it. Every new choice is in **Settings → Advanced**, on phones only. Each one starts on **Classic**, the way the app has always worked, and it shows only on a phone whose app carries the Rust part (every release since 0.17.0 does). If the Rust part fails at anything, the classic code takes over at once, and the app counts each time this happens.

- **Torrents: Director** (Settings → Advanced → Torrent engine → Director). When you watch a torrent while it downloads, Director plans which pieces come first. It fetches the end of the file, where players look for the index, together with its start. It keeps a stretch ahead of you that grows when the peers give more than the video needs and shrinks when they give less, and it sets a deadline for each piece just ahead of where you are. A seek drops the old plan at once. Downloading itself, the peers and seeding stay with the classic engine. The figures come from a simulated swarm on a computer, not from real peers or a phone:
  - on a fast swarm the first frame came after 3 s instead of 11 s;
  - on a slow one it came after 8 s instead of 78 s;
  - seeks were as quick as before;
  - on the slow swarm it paused for 0.3 s where the classic rules did not.

  A torrent keeps the engine it was added with.
- **The screen translator** (Settings → Advanced → Screen translator engine → Rust). Three jobs move to Rust:
  - following the words as the page scrolls, so each box stays on its line;
  - working out how far the screen moved between two pictures;
  - cleaning the text off a picture in «erase» mode.

  Measured on a computer, they take between a quarter and four fifths of the time they took before. **New:** a picture already cleaned is remembered, so a speech balloon read again costs a tenth of a clean or less.

  Some parts stay on Android's own code: reading the text from the screen, drawing the boxes with their fonts, capturing the screen and the translations themselves. Fetching translations ahead and the lettering rules stay too, because they run faster where they are. Videos that arrive as many small pieces (HLS) are read by Rust too when Download engine is Rust.
- **File jobs** (Settings → Advanced → File jobs engine → Rust). Copy, move and delete in Files are planned by Rust and carried out by the app as before. Every step that deletes or overwrites is first checked against the classic plan. An item on which the two plans disagree is skipped and listed, never touched, and every step looks at the disk again before it acts.
  - Each job is written down as it goes. A job that was cut short (the app closed, the phone restarted, the card pulled out) can be **resumed** or **rolled back**: Settings → Advanced → Rust core status → Interrupted jobs.
  - A finished job made only of copies, moves, renames and new folders can be undone. The app keeps what it needs and shows how many such jobs there are. The button to undo one from the Files page comes in a later release.
- **Rust core status** at the end of Settings → Advanced. It shows:
  - the Rust part's version;
  - each engine in use and the one you chose;
  - how many times each one handed work back to the classic code;
  - on test builds, how often its answers were compared with the classic ones and how many differed;
  - the Director's plans;
  - the screen translator's picture memory;
  - the file jobs that can be resumed or undone.

  **Copy report** puts it all on the clipboard as plain text, to send to us. It holds no passwords, keys or sign-ins.
- **Settings → Advanced is tidied:** one row per engine, in the same shape, with a one-line hint and «in use · handed back» under each. On the download engine, «Java» is now called **Classic**, like the others.

### Not in this release
- **A torrent engine written wholly in Rust.** It now builds for all three phone processor types, but it is held back because it does not yet finish a download in its own test. It would add about 2–2.7 MB to each phone file.

### What we ask you to try
On a phone, pick **Rust** or **Director** in Settings → Advanced, use the app as usual for a few days, then send us **Copy report** from Rust core status, whether or not anything went wrong:
- **Director:** open a torrent video at once, seek far ahead and back, and compare how soon it plays with Classic.
- **Screen translator:** a long comic in auto mode (scroll slowly, fling, scroll back), a chat with changing numbers, and «erase» mode on speech balloons.
- **File jobs:** a big move to an SD card or USB stick, a copy where names clash, a delete. Close the app in the middle of one, then resume it or roll it back.

The number handed back to Classic should stay at 0. Switch back to Classic if anything looks wrong.
