### Fixes
- **Back no longer goes round between two tabs** (Android). From a page reached through a tab switch, Back went to the other tab, then back to the page, and so on, both with the phone's Back and the ← at the top. Now ← goes up within its own tab (a show back to the list). The phone's Back closes what is open, then goes up, then to Downloads, and from Downloads' top sends the app to the background.
- **A download's page no longer slides sideways.** A long magnet link made its card thousands of pixels wide, and the page scrolled off into emptiness. The name now wraps to three lines and the address is cut to one.
- **«Peers · seeds» counts peers without the seeds.** libtorrent counts the seeds among the peers, so both numbers read 35 when everyone connected was a seed.
- «1 segments» now reads «1 segment», in Arabic too.

### New
- **A torrent up close** (Android): a Swarm card on a torrent's page.
  - It shows the seeds and peers connected and how many the swarm has, the peers sending and receiving now (their clients and progress), and every tracker with how it answered. It refreshes every two seconds.
  - **Check the files again** verifies what is on the disk.
  - **Find peers now** asks every tracker and the DHT at once.
  - Each download can have **its own speed limits**.
- **Watching while downloading starts sooner** (Seanime's order). The video's first 8 MB come first, then the next 24 MB, and its last 4 MB early too, where a video keeps the index a player reads to seek.
- **The next episode is fetched while you watch** (Seanime, Hayase). Halfway through an episode, the next one's best release starts downloading; from a batch, only its file. Turn it off in Anime settings.
- **Connection limits** (Hayase's): connections per torrent (80) and in all (500), in Torrent options.
- **Install an app** from the + menu: an APK, a bundle, or an archive with an app inside, picked from the phone and read where it is. Nothing is copied first.
- **Apps among a torrent's files** can be installed from the download's menu, one row for each app.
- **Watching while downloading keeps a buffer**: about 24 MB ahead of what plays, on rising deadlines. While you watch, the other torrents slow to a trickle, and they get their speed back a minute after the player stops.
- **Faster connections**: TCP is preferred over µTP when a peer has both (µTP backs off sooner and is often slower on phones), and socket buffers are bigger.
- **Watched episodes** (Settings, with the Anime or TV add-on), like Hayase's storage choices. After watching, an episode's download is kept, deleted when the player closes, or deleted some days later. The card shows the room episodes take, and deletes the watched ones, or all of them, at once.
- **Hold an episode** (Anime, TV) for its options: watch, download, choose a release, mark it watched or not, mark every one up to it watched, see its download's details, or delete its download.
- **AniList in Settings**: its own card near the top when the Anime add-on is installed.
- **Subtitle languages in Settings** (with the Subtitles or Player add-on), in the order you want them; tap one to put it first. The player now shows the subtitle in your first language that is there by itself, read from the track's tag or its file name (`.ar.srt`, `[Arabic]`, `eng`), unless you chose one by hand.
