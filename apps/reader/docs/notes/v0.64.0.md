### Fixes
- **Justified text no longer opens wide gaps between words.** With a large font on a narrow screen, a short line was stretched from margin to margin. The new **Smart justify** (now the default wherever text was justified) keeps the even margins and sets a paragraph ragged only when its gaps would grow too wide. The gaps are measured on your screen, with your font and size.
- **«Next volume», then Back, no longer bounces between the two volumes.** Back now asks: **Back to the previous volume** (named, and reopened where you left it), **Main page**, or **Stay here**. The same holds when the next volume opens by scrolling past the end.
- **The arrow at the top of a book closes what is open first**, like the phone's Back. It used to leave the book at once, even with a panel open.
- **On a phone, Back on a picture opened to full view closes it.** It used to step to the previous picture.
- **Esc no longer closes two things at once** (in the gallery, the picture view, speed reading, the Anki review, renaming, the PDF pages grid).
- **Back leaves full screen on computers**, and stops auto-scroll or reading aloud before it leaves the book.

### New: text spacing in your hands
*Visual Options → Alignment* is no longer locked by Book formatting, and has five choices:
- **Book's own**: as the book sets each paragraph.
- **Smart justify**: justified, without the wide gaps.
- **Justified**: always to both margins.
- **Natural** and **Centred**.

Beside it:
- **Hyphenation** has its own switch, apart from Book formatting. A book that does not state its language but reads as English is hyphenated as English, whatever the app's language.
- **Word spacing, letter spacing and paragraph indent** work with every alignment, as before.
- Every change here can be undone and redone from the top of the sheet.
- If you had Book formatting on, or Justified chosen, you are moved to Smart justify once. Choose **Justified** to have the old behaviour back.

### New: «Use book formatting» for Text as image
The Text as image sheet has a new switch. **On**, the picture is set as the book sets the text: separate paragraphs, its alignment, first-line indent, bold, italics and sizes. **Off**, plain text in your reading font, as before. Your choice is remembered.

### New: one Back for the whole app
The arrow, the phone's Back button and gesture, Esc, Alt+← and the mouse's back button now all do the same thing, in one order: what is open on top closes first, then full screen, then auto-scroll or reading aloud, then selected text, then the next-volume question, then the book.

**Changed on computers: Esc now leaves the book when nothing else is open.** It used to stop at the book. While you type in a field, Esc only clears or leaves that field. *Back twice to leave a book* guards Esc as it guards the phone's Back; the arrow still leaves at once.

### What we ask you to try
- **Smart justify on a phone**, with a large font, in an English book and an Arabic one. Phones hyphenate English, so fewer paragraphs should fall back to ragged there than in our tests.
- **Next volume, then Back**, with the arrow and with the gesture.
