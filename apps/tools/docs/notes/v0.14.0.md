### New
- **mpv, the engine of mpv-android, mpvEx and REX Player, in the Player add-on (Android).** The Player add-on now downloads mpv with it, as a pack, so the APK stays the same size. When mpv is there, videos play through it, under the player's own controls, gestures and subtitles.
  - **Every format plays:** AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS and TrueHD sound, and HEVC and 10-bit video, decoded by the phone's hardware where it can.
  - **Subtitles inside the file are found and listed** in the subtitles menu under «In the video itself». That includes styled ASS, drawn by libass, and picture subtitles.
  - **Preferred languages:** set your preferred subtitle and audio languages in *Settings → Player*. The file's tracks in those languages are picked by themselves when it opens.
  - **You can switch it off:** *Settings → Player → Playback engine* goes back to the browser's player.
- **Every tool of REX Player's legend.** A new *Tools* button on the player's bar opens a grid of all of them:
  - chapters and bookmarks, playback speed, decoder, screen rotation, frame navigation, video zoom;
  - picture-in-picture, aspect ratio, lock, audio track, subtitles, current chapter;
  - repeat, shuffle, horizontal and vertical flip, A-B loop, custom skip;
  - background playback, ambient mode, sleep timer, video quality, cut, screenshot, bookmarks, jump to a time and media info.
- **Player layout.** Choose which tools sit on the player's bar and in what order. Open *Tools → Player layout*, tick the ones you want, and move them up or down.
- **Scissors on the bar (cut).** The cut sheet opens straight from the scissors. Set each end by typing its time, by tapping *Point A here* or *Point B here* at the current moment, or by nudging it a frame or a second at a time.
- **Frame navigation.** Step back or on by one frame or ten; with mpv each step is an exact frame. The panel shows the frame's number, time and rate. You can save the frame as a picture (mpv saves it in *Pictures/Kurokawa*), make it A or B of a cut, or open the cut.
- **Decoder (mpv).** Choose hardware, hardware with copying (works with filters and screenshots), or software. The file reopens at the same place. Debanding and the newer gpu-next renderer can be turned on in *Settings → Player → Decoder*.
- **Audio settings.** Set your preferred audio languages, and choose audio channels as in the file or down-mixed to stereo.

### Fixes
- **Fewer torrent crashes.** If libtorrent crashed on your phone last time, the app now switches to its simple disk access by itself when it starts again. Crashes like this were seen as heap corruption on libtorrent's disk threads, on some Android 16–17 phones. The fast disk access also uses fewer threads than before.
