### Fixes
- **Anime and torrents on a computer said they need «the app on a phone or computer»**: when the download engine (aria2c) was slow to start — as on a first start while Windows' antivirus checks it — the app gave up after six seconds and fell back to the browser's downloader, which can't download torrents and only handed links over (and called them done). The computer now always uses its own engine: it waits for aria2c up to half a minute, starts it again by itself if it stops, and any download you add starts it if it isn't running. The window no longer waits for it to open.
- When the engine can't start, Downloads says why in its own words, with **Try again**.

### Computer
- **Right-click menus**: right-click a download, a file in Files, an episode or a video for its menu at the pointer; the ⋮ buttons open the same small menu under themselves, and choice buttons drop down under themselves — no more dialogs in the middle of the screen for a few options. A long press on a phone still chooses.
- Downloads' empty page now points at the sidebar's «Add a download», dropping a link on the window, and Ctrl+V.

### Icons
- **An icon of your choosing**: pick any picture and crop it — move it, zoom (wheel, pinch or slider), turn it, see it round, rounded or square, and fill what it leaves bare with a colour.
  - **The app's own icon** (Settings → General): on a computer the window, the taskbar, the Dock and the shortcuts show it, kept across starts, the original back with one tap; on Android a home-screen shortcut with it.
  - **An installed app's icon in Files**, changed the same way.
