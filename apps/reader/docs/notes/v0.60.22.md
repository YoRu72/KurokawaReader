### Fixes — PDF → Word, fonts
- **Far fewer fonts in books that quote the Quran.** Such books carry one font per mushaf page; a tested 1,061-page book came out with 286 fonts in Word's list. The words of those fonts are now gathered into a few fonts («QCF Merged 1…»), each word drawn exactly as before: 33 fonts instead of 286, and the Word file 3 MB smaller.
- **Signs and ornaments that drew nothing.** Symbol fonts (the basmalah sign, ornaments, Arabic symbols) lost their signs when their pieces were joined into one font. They are drawn again.
- **Fonts left out of the file.** Some fonts were neither embedded nor listed (for example «Droid Arabic Naskh», «louts shamy»), so Word replaced them without saying. They are embedded now.
- **A missing letter.** A font carried into the file while lacking a letter the text uses (a «خ» in one tested book) made those words fall to another font and break their joining. Such a font is now rebuilt with all its letters.
- **Every font the text uses is listed in the file**, with what Word needs to choose a fitting replacement when a font is not embedded.

### Known
- A heading set in a decorative font whose PDF gives no text for it keeps the PDF's code as its text (it shows correctly through the embedded font).
