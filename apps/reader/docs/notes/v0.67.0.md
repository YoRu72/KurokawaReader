## Fixed

- **PDF to Word: tables drawn with thin rules lost their words.** A table ruled with hairlines that do not touch (as typesetting programs draw them) was taken for a chart and its text vanished from the result. Such tables are now tables, and a check runs on every conversion test: every word of the PDF must be in the Word file.
- **PDF to Word: a footnote could land in the page footer** and repeat on every page. Notes are never taken for the running foot now.
- **Glossary: the *Install* button's text ran outside the button on the phone.** The tick is gone and the word fits.
- **Glossary: the same term in several spellings** (upper and lower case, full and half width, with and without Arabic marks, hiragana and katakana) counted as different terms. They are one term now; the other spellings are kept as alternatives.
- **Translation bar on narrow phones:** its buttons were smaller than a fingertip and touched each other, notices covered it, and the *Add a term* label was cut.
- **Browse:** «Checked 20,782 days ago» was shown when nothing had been checked yet; search fields showed two clear buttons; the switch for updating extensions by themselves could not be reached while nothing was followed.
- **Arabic interface: words ran together.** The interface font's space is narrow; words now have a proper gap. The book's own text is not touched.
- **An Arabic reading font that drew nothing.** «المهند» held no Arabic letters, so text fell back to another font without saying so. The option is gone; whoever had chosen it gets «عثمان».
- **Notices covered open sheets.** A notice such as *Adding 1 file…* now sits above an open bottom sheet instead of on its controls.
- **Buttons of unequal width** in the delete dialog and the highlight sheet; cut labels in the selection bar and the yearly-goal field; the *Keep pictures* switch glued to its label.
- **Settings:** a tap on a section's chip scrolled the section's heading under the bar.
- **Memory:** the notes page and the library each left one listener behind on every visit.

## New: Comics
- **A reader made for comics.** Five reading modes — left to right, right to left, vertical, long strip, continuous vertical — remembered per book, with a default of your choice. Pinch and double-tap zoom, six ways to fit a page, tap zones in five layouts (with an overlay that shows them), keyboard and mouse on the PC, volume keys on the phone, a page slider that runs the way you read, and a page between volumes that opens the next one.
- **Wide pages** can be split in two, turned to use the height, or two pages shown side by side (always, or on wide screens only, with a one-page shift for books whose pairs are off).
- **Crop borders**, a **colour filter** (brightness, tint, greyscale, inverted), a background of your choice, and a page's own menu on long press: save, share, copy, set as the cover.
- **Long strips** are drawn in slices, so very tall pages stay sharp and memory stays low. Only the pages near the one you read are kept in memory, whatever the size of the file.
- **CBR, CB7 and CBT files** (RAR, 7z and tar archives of pictures) open too. They are repacked once when added, with progress and Cancel; a protected archive asks for its password. A file is recognised by its content, not its name.

## New: PDF to Word
- **A document you can edit.** The converter used to copy the page line by line. The new default, *Easy to edit*, writes real paragraphs (also across a page break), headings that Word's navigation pane knows, bulleted and numbered lists, working links, pictures in the text beside their captions, table header rows and real footnotes. *Exact look* keeps the old line-by-line copy.
- *Exact look* is better too for PDFs made by LaTeX and by Chrome: lines no longer break one word early, and the page count matches the PDF.
- The converter is now measured against the same document made into PDF by twelve programs; no change may make any of them worse.

## New: AI translator
- **«Would you like to gather the terms?»** Before a translation starts, the Reader offers to collect the book's names and terms into its glossary first, with *Do not ask again for this book*. When an earlier volume already has a glossary, it is checked and the notice says how many new terms were added.
- **Start by itself** (off by default): the translator may begin when a book opens; each book can differ.
- **Export the translation** as a separate EPUB, in one language or both. The translation inside the book stays as it was.

## New: Glossary
- **Names in the text.** The glossary's terms are marked where they appear in the book; a tap shows the term's card. Two switches in the glossary's options turn the marks and the card on and off.
- **Search across languages:** a search finds a term by any of its renderings, whatever language you type in.
- **Delete fully:** a folder, a file or everything, with Undo.

## New: Library
- **Edit several books at once:** author and series for a selection.
- **A book's language** can be set and filtered by.
- **Search your notes** across all books.
- **A yearly reading goal** on the statistics page.

## New: Browse
- **Updates only when there is something to update.** The bell appears once a work you follow has news, with a count; its sheet lists every followed work and can check the others. The settings section appears with the first work you follow and has *Check now*.
- **Search your extensions by name** across all the repositories you added, with filters for installed, updates and language.

## Not checked on a real device

- CBR files: only a small RAR 4 archive made here was tried. RAR 5, compressed RAR and protected RAR or 7z archives were not. A very large archive may be refused with a message, since it is held in memory while it is repacked.
- The comic reader's touch gestures (pinch, pan, swipes), the rotation lock, brightness and volume keys were tested with simulated input only.
- Marked glossary names were tested with simulated taps.
- PDF to Word was measured on PDFs made here by twelve programs. PDFs from Word, InDesign, Google Docs and Acrobat could not be made here.
- Still wrong in PDF to Word: a PDF made by wkhtmltopdf gains a page and its table is cut at the page end; Arabic headings of some LaTeX PDFs come out as wrong letters (the PDF itself lacks them).
- Scanned PDFs still come out as pictures: the Reader has no text recognition.
