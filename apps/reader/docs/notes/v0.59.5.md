### Fixes
- **OTL finds a series by any of its names.** OTL's series are linked to AniList, so a search now also matches a series' other titles there: its romaji, English and native names and its synonyms. Searching *Kusuriya no Hitorigoto* finds *The Apothecary Diaries*. Names you search for are asked of AniList once, and each series' names are kept with its tags.

### New
- **A new logo.** Kurokawa Reader has its own icon: an open book, in the same style as Kurokawa Tools. It is used on Android (adaptive and themed icons included), iOS, the computer apps and the web app.
- **Faster downloads.** Chapters, books and updates are downloaded over more connections at once (16 by default, up to 24). Like speed-test apps, a download starts with a few connections and adds more for as long as each addition makes it faster, then stops. A server that refuses more connections is no longer pushed.
