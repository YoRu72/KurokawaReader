### Fixes
- **DNS over HTTPS made to hold up on a network that fights back.** It used to ask one resolver, one way, and give up when that was refused — which is why it often "did nothing". Now:
  - **Eight resolvers** (Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, AdGuard, Mullvad, DNS.SB, ControlD, OpenDNS, or your own): the chosen one first, the others when it does not answer; the one that answered last is asked first next time. **Automatic** picks the fastest that answers.
  - **Each resolver asked in several shapes** until one gets through — by its own address (no DNS at all), as a POST, with the TLS hello split in two, as a GET, by the address alone without the name in the hello, and the JSON form by name as a last resort. The shape that worked is remembered per resolver.
  - **The TLS hello split** for the sites themselves: the start of the secure connection is sent in two records with the cut inside the site's name, so a filter that reads names from the first packet reads none. On for blocked sites by default; "Always" and "Off" in Settings.
  - **A site the network blocks even when its address is right** (the connection reset or timed out, the handshake refused): the browser forces it through the resolver with the split hello and loads it again by itself; a download does the same after its two usual tries.
  - The status card and the browser's DoH chip now say **which resolver answered, by which shape, in how long**, list every resolver with how it last fared, and the Test button tries them all at once.
- **The translator** (the screen translator, the player's subtitle translation, the overlay): Google is asked **many lines in one request** instead of one request a line — a subtitle file translates in a few requests rather than hundreds, and far fewer "too many requests" refusals; **Microsoft Translator** added as a second free engine (no key; up to a hundred lines a request); **Google and Microsoft stand in for each other** when one refuses, for a minute at a time. Same on the phone's floating translator.

### Browser
- **Ad and tracker blocking** (on by default; the menu turns it off and counts what was blocked) — the pop-under scripts of download sites with it.
- A **forward** button beside back.
- **Hold a link**: open it, download it with the app, copy it, share it.
- **Full-screen video** (the page's own full-screen button works; Back leaves it).
- Turning DoH on from the browser picks Automatic.
