### Fixes
- **Downloads keep going when you leave the app.** In the background, the app kept sending the whole download list to its hidden page every second. On some phones this piled up until Android reported the app as not responding and closed it, stopping every download while its notification still showed. The list is now held while the app is in the background and sent once when you come back.
- **Downloads keep their speed with the screen off.** While downloads run, the phone stays awake enough for them (the processor, and Wi-Fi at full speed). This is let go as soon as nothing is downloading.
- **Lighter notification updates.** The progress notification is refreshed at most once a second.

If downloads still stop on your phone, set Kurokawa Tools to *Unrestricted* in Android’s battery settings for the app (*App info → Battery*). Some phones (realme, Xiaomi and others) stop background apps on their own otherwise.
