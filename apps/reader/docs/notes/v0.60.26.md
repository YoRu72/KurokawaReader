### Fixes — search in the book, read-aloud, notes export
- **Search in the book finds phrases that cross formatting.** A phrase partly in bold or italic, across a link, written with ruby (furigana), or crossing the edge of a highlight was never found. It is found now, in Arabic too, and opening the result marks exactly those words. A match never joins the end of one paragraph to the start of the next.
- **PDF "Speak" stops when the book is closed**, and pressing it again stops the voice already speaking.
- **Stopping read-aloud right after starting it** no longer lets one passage be spoken.
- **"Export notes" includes PDF page notes**, in the notes file and in the Anki export.
