### Fixes
- **The app no longer closes by itself while a torrent is being checked or downloaded** (Android). The report from 0.9.2 showed a native crash (SIGSEGV) a moment into «Checking». The torrent engine read and wrote files by mapping them into memory, and on the phone's shared storage a mapped page that cannot be read ends the whole app. It now reads and writes files the ordinary way. A torrent that kept restarting its check finishes it and goes on downloading.
- **«Cannot access 'b' before initialization»** no longer appears. It came from tapping the **+** button while its menu was already open.
- **Servers that cut connections** (DDoS-Guard and similar, as on pawchive): a connection the server resets now drops out and hands its part to the connections still going, instead of failing the download. If even one connection is cut, the error says why and offers **Open in browser**: open the link once there, the site's check passes, and the download reuses it.
- **Settings**: each hint sits under its label instead of running into it, number fields are compact and beside their label, and nothing shows through above the search bar while scrolling.
- A link or magnet shared to the app while it is already open now opens the Add sheet (it was ignored).

### New
- **Choose the files of a batch.** A batch (or any torrent, with the list button beside it) shows its files before downloading. The episode is read from each name, extras (creditless openings and endings, previews, samples) are left unticked, and there are quick choices: *this episode only*, *episodes only*, *all*, *none*, or a range *from episode … to …*. «Download the best release» and automatic downloads take only the wanted episodes from a batch. A range download that uses a batch skips the episodes you already have and the fillers. The Add sheet's file list works the same way.
- **A post's photos and videos by its link.** Paste a post from X (x.com, twitter.com, fxtwitter, fixupx, vxtwitter, nitter), Bluesky or Reddit into the grabber, or into the Add sheet. You get its media at full quality: original-size photos and the best MP4. Files are named after the post, with thumbnails to choose from. Video streams (Bluesky, Reddit) go to the Video add-on.
- **The grabber finds videos.** It now finds the page's players and their sources, the video in its sharing tags (og:video) and structured data, and video addresses written in its scripts. The app's browser also catches what players load even without a file extension, by what the server says it is or because a video element asked for it. Stream playlists (HLS, DASH) go to the Video add-on, which joins them. With the Video add-on installed, **Find the page's video** hands the page to it.
