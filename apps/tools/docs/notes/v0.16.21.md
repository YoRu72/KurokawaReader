### Fixes
- **Files: the storage buttons spilled out of their row** with large text on a phone («Internal storage» cut off under «Kurokawa», covering the line above). They now sit in one row that scrolls sideways, a little more compact.

### Your own subtitle font
- **Subtitle font**, in the player's subtitle style (and in the player's settings): pick a font file of your own (.ttf, .otf, .woff) and it becomes the main subtitle font. The list shows each font written in itself; fonts can be removed from there too.
- **Plain subtitles** (SRT, VTT, subtitles found online, a translated track) are drawn in it.
- **Styled subtitles too** if you turn on «In styled subtitles too»: ASS files and the video's own subtitles take your font in every style, while signs and on-screen text keep their own fonts. This works with the computer's player and with mpv on Android.
- **Clips** cut with the subtitles keep your font.
