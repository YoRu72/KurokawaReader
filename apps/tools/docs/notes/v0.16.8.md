### Fixes
- **«From the clipboard» said «The clipboard holds no link» on phones**: the + menu and the sheet's Paste read the web view's clipboard, which Android refuses. They now read the phone's clipboard through the app, as the clipboard watcher always did, so a copied X or Instagram link is found.
- **A post read by yt-dlp still showed a file's parts**: under the qualities it said «this link gives a web page, not a file» and offered a file name. A page yt-dlp reads now shows only its qualities.
- **Open opened the folder, not the file**: since 0.14.8 Open on a finished download went to the file browser. It opens the file in its app again; «Show in folder» still shows the folder.
- **The screen stayed on its side after leaving the player**: closing the player now puts the screen back to what the phone does by itself.
- **Taps outside a sheet over the player did nothing**: the sheet's backdrop sat under the player. A tap anywhere outside now closes it.

### New download
- **A clear button** in the link field: the link, a chosen .torrent and what was found for them, gone at once.
- **A post's photos and videos open at once** (X, Instagram, Bluesky, Reddit, Telegram…), without tapping «Show them». It can be turned off in Settings → Downloads.
- **Share** on a finished download: in its menu, its «Download complete» card and its details.

### Player
- **REX Player's floating frame controls**: the Frames button puts a small pad on the video — a frame back, save the frame, a frame on (hold for ten), close; A, clear, B and Cut — no menu each time. Tap its time for the full panel.
- **Floating window (picture in picture)** on Android: by its button, or by itself when you leave the app while a video plays (Player settings → Playback can turn that off).
- **Screen rotation on the bar**, with Frames and the floating window, for bars set up before.
- **Compact sheets**: a side panel when the phone is on its side, smaller rows; chapter times lined up at the end.

### Files
- **The drawer from the side**, as in Solid Explorer: swipe from the side to open it, swipe it back to close it.
- **The gallery**: swipe between the folder's pictures and GIFs, the picture following your finger; its videos are among them, each with a Play button.

### Search
- **Faster**: the sources load when the page opens, the same search again is shown at once (kept ten minutes), the covers of what you type appear while you type, and three covers are matched at a time.
- **Nyaa 0.2.0**: a picked work is asked for by all its names in one request, with its episode, in the anime category; trusted and remake marks; a mirror, a category and a filter in its settings. The Anime tab's Nyaa asks once instead of one name after another; Torrentio and EZTV ask at once.
