### PDF → Word: tables and vowel marks
- **Tables drawn by Word or LibreOffice came apart**: their borders are drawn in short pieces, and pieces a few points long were ignored. The table wasn't seen, so its columns were read as columns of the page and the cells spilled out as loose text. Short border pieces now count, and a table's columns no longer pass for the page's columns.
- **A table's lower rows were taken for footnotes** when one of its borders looked like a footnote rule. A border is no longer taken for a footnote rule.
- **Harakat (vowel marks) were lost** in books whose fonts tell PDF readers that every mark is a space (KFGQPC's fonts among them). The marks are now read from the font's own map, so they come through in Word, and in copying and searching too.
- On a 27-page Arabic book full of tables, the result went from 42 pages to 38 in LibreOffice, with the tables whole. Word, the reference for right-to-left layout, wasn't available to compare against.
