### Fixes
- **OTL genres and tags showed only for some series:** they were looked up only once the folder's index was made. Now every series is looked up, those on the screen first, and a series' page shows "Looking up genres and tags…" until its own arrive.
- **Tag search didn't show every series:** tapping a tag or genre now filters the shelf by it — every series tagged so, not a search for the word in names. The tags picked show over the shelf (a tap takes one off), and the shelf fills in while AniList is still answering.
- **Slow downloads:** a site behind Cloudflare sent every file through the browser, one slow connection at a time. Files now come over 8 connections with the browser's cookies, as Mihon does; only a site that refuses that still goes the slow way. Downloading a long novel no longer slows down as it grows, and a site asking to slow down is waited for instead of failing the chapter.
- **Broken chapters:** a chapter the site gave as a "Just a moment…" / "Verify you are human" page, or as nothing, is no longer saved as the chapter. One saved that way before is fetched again.
- **AniList progress made offline is no longer lost:** it's saved on the book and sent when the network comes back.
- **PDF → Word:**
  - Verse lines no longer break in two when opened in LibreOffice: each half is fitted to its space by the width its letters really take, kashida included.
  - Brackets came out back to front in some Arabic books: «)لَا يكونُ خبرا(» instead of «(لَا يكونُ خبرا)». A bracket's glyph is now chosen by the shape it draws.
  - Verse numbers like «(١)» no longer turn into «)١)».
  - References such as «(١٩٧/٦)» keep the order the page shows.
  - Honorific signs such as «رحمه الله» drawn by a sign font no longer show as «$».
  - English names inside Arabic lines keep their order: «John Smith», not «Smith John».

### Faster and lighter
- The app is about 1.3 MB smaller (the sample book's pictures, same look).
- Long novels and AniList tags are written to storage less often, so reading and browsing stay smooth.

### New
- **Covers at full quality:** the cover viewer shows the cover from the book's own file (or AniList's biggest) instead of the shelf's small copy.
- **Read aloud with the screen off (Android):** reading goes on behind other apps and with the screen off, with a media notification — play/pause, next, previous, stop — and your headset's buttons.
- **Move a novel to another source:** when its site dies or is blocked, pick the same novel in another extension. Your place, read and starred chapters, bookmarks and offline chapters move with it. On the novel's page and the book's page.
- **Smarter update checks:** check a novel only near its next chapter (by how often its chapters come out), and skip novels with chapters still to read or not started. In the updates settings.
- **Discover** on the Browse page: trending, most popular and best-rated light novels from AniList; a tap searches all your sources for it.
- **Translation glossary:** names and terms the AI translator always renders the same way, for one book or every book. Select a name, then More → Add to the translation glossary.
- **Remove or replace text everywhere:** select words (a site's watermark, a name you want spelt your way), then More → Remove or replace everywhere. Read-aloud and search follow the rules. Manage them in Settings → Text rules.
- **Automatic backups:** your reading and settings are kept on the device every day (or every few days), the last few saved. Restoring lets you pick what to bring back: settings, reading, books, fonts.
- **App lock (Android):** your fingerprint, face or screen lock to open the app, and on coming back after a while. Can also hide the app's contents from screenshots and recent apps. In Settings → Privacy.
- **Incognito mode:** what you read isn't added to the history or reading stats and isn't sent to AniList. In the History or Settings → Privacy.
- **Open a shared link:** share a novel's page, a Shamela book or an archive.org item to the app (Android), or paste it into "Search all sources" or "Import from link", and it opens in the source that has it.
- **Duplicate warning:** a novel's page says when the same series is already in your library from another source, and asks before adding it again.
- **"Browser" button in the "Confirm you're human" window (Android):** opens the site in the app's full browser when the check won't pass in the window.
