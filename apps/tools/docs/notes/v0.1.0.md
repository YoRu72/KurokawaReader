### The first release
- **Downloads:** links (several at once, pasted or typed), magnet links, .torrent files; the folder, the name, the connections; a torrent's files picked before it starts.
- **Engines:** on Android the app's own — libtorrent for torrents (DHT, encryption, sequential download, streaming while it comes), eight connections for plain files, a notification while they run, everything resumed after a restart; on Windows and Linux, aria2c beside the app; on macOS, Homebrew's aria2c when installed; anywhere, a remote aria2 you point the app at (Settings → Remote aria2).
- **The list:** progress, speed, peers and seeds, what is left; pause, resume, retry, remove (with or without the files); filters; a page per download with its files, each to download, skip or put first.
- **Settings:** language (Arabic, English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese), theme and accent (the reader's Material 3 look), download folder, speed limits, seeding, listening port.
- **Add-ons:** the list from the Kurokawa repository (empty for now — torrent search, anime, subtitles, debrid, archives and the player come as add-ons in the next releases).
- **Other Kurokawa apps:** KurokawaReader opened when installed, or fetched and installed from here with its progress.
- **Updates:** the app checks the repository's `tools-v*` releases and installs them (Android, Windows, Linux).
