### Fixes
- The reader no longer appears in the list of apps when you tap an APK (or any other unknown file) to install it: it now offers itself only for the kinds it reads.

### New
- **Archives** (a new button in the library's tools, in the apps): open a zip, 7z, rar, tar or gzip/xz/bzip2 archive to see inside and extract it — beside the archive, into a folder of its name or elsewhere; replace, skip or keep both; passwords; progress with cancel. A ZIP can be extracted **in place**: files come out from the end of the archive and the archive is cut back after each batch, so the disk only needs room for the files; a stopped run is continued later. Zips are made from files you pick (pictures and other packed files stored as they are).
- **Other Kurokawa apps** (Settings): Kurokawa Tools opened when it is on the device, or fetched from the releases and installed from here with its progress.
