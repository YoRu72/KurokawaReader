### Fixes
- **Tapping a picture in a chapter opens it** (EPUB), to look closer, save, copy or share, in more books than before:
  - **A picture inside a link** opens instead of following the link. Books often link their pictures. A small picture inside a line of text, such as a footnote's mark, still follows its link.
  - **A page illustration wrapped in SVG** opens too. This is how most light novels place a full-page picture. It is now shown as a whole page and listed in the book's pictures.
- **Pictures that were missing now show:**
  - Pictures given only as a `srcset`, or inside a `<picture>`, now load; the sharpest candidate is taken.
  - Pictures placed with an `<object>` or `<embed>` now show.
  - A chapter made only of pictures is no longer skipped when reading chapter by chapter.
