### Fixes
- **Holding a file didn't select it**: on a phone, holding an item in Files lit it up, then letting go unselected it again. The hold now selects it, and the tap that comes with letting go is ignored. The same fix applies everywhere a hold is used.
- **A title cut to «Dowr…»**: when a page's top bar has more buttons than it has room for (large system text, an add-on's extra button), the title now moves to its own line under the buttons instead of being cut. If the buttons still don't fit, they scroll instead of pushing the page wider.
- **Filter chips hidden under the status card** (Downloads, phone): the chips keep their full height and stay at the top while you scroll the list.
- **Zipped subtitles wouldn't load**: subtitle sites often send a .zip (sometimes a whole season), and the player failed with «unknown format». It now opens the .zip or .gz itself: every subtitle in it becomes a track, and the one for the episode playing is turned on. Automatic subtitles can use these too now.
- **.ass files greyed out in the phone's file picker**: «Subtitles → From a file» now lets you pick any file, since Android has no type for .ass/.ssa.

### Subtitles in the player
- **Search in your own words**: «Find subtitles» now has a search box and an episode field, filled in from the file's name. Change either one and search again.
- **No subtitle sites yet?** The search now says so and offers a button that takes you straight to adding them, instead of a message that disappears.
- **Drop a subtitle onto the player** (computer): drag a .srt, .ass, .vtt or a .zip from a subtitle site onto the video and it's loaded and turned on.

### TV & Movies
- **Schedule**, as in Anime: this week's new episodes day by day (streaming services and US channels, from TVmaze), each with its time, picture, season and episode, and how long until it airs. Shows on your list or that you're watching come first, and a «Yours» chip shows only those. Tap one to open the show at that season.

### The player on a computer
- **Volume on the bar**: a speaker button (mute) with a slider that opens on hover.
- **A cleaner look**: deeper shading behind the top and bottom bars, buttons that light up on hover, and glassy chips.
- **Frames and clips**:
  - Frames are saved **without** subtitles by default.
  - A subtitles button on the frame pad switches this on or off, and the frame panel has the same choice. Turned on, the subtitles are drawn in their own fonts.
  - When cutting a clip, «Burn the subtitles in (with their own fonts)» is now off unless you turn it on.

### Keyboard (computer)
- **Files**:

  | Key | Does |
  |---|---|
  | Ctrl+A | Select all |
  | Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V | Copy / cut / paste |
  | Ctrl+F | Filter |
  | Delete | Delete |
  | F2 | Rename |
  | Esc | Leave the selection |
  | Alt+↑ or Backspace | Up a folder |

  With two panes, the keys act on the one under the pointer.
