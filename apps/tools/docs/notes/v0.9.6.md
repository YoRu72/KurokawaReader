### Fixes
- **Torrents download at full speed again** (Android). Since 0.9.3 the torrent engine used libtorrent's simple disk access, a wrong guess at the crash that 0.9.5 actually fixed. That mode does every read, write and check on the network's own thread, so while a torrent was checked or written, peers were left waiting and downloads crawled. The engine is back on libtorrent's fast disk access, on its own threads. The simple one stays as a choice in Settings › Downloads › Torrent options, for storage that misbehaves with the fast one.
- **A magnet's details come much sooner.** Well-known public trackers are added to every public torrent (never to a private one). The list is refreshed daily from the trackers that answer. Every tracker is told about the torrent at once, instead of one group after another. The DHT remembers its nodes between starts, so it asks peers right away instead of starting cold. Turn the trackers off in Torrent options if you prefer.
- **Faster connections:** more connection attempts a second, a bigger boost for a newly added torrent, a shorter wait on peers that do not answer, deeper request queues, and more disk and hashing threads.
- **Downloads are no longer forced into order.** Anime, TV and automatic downloads asked for every piece in order, which leaves most peers unable to help. They now take the rarest pieces first, like other torrent apps. Watching while downloading still gets the pieces the player needs first. «Download in order» is now off by default.
