### Fixes
- On a computer, the video choices made in the Add sheet now all reach yt-dlp: subtitles, the cover, SponsorBlock and playlist numbering were dropped before.
- Colours set per item now show; among them the anime page's cover colour and the new connection lanes.
- Time left reads as a clock (3:15) in every language, instead of «4 min» in English.
- On a phone, the Downloads title fits again: pause all, resume all, clear finished, Cloud and Settings are under ⋮.
- An explicit «Add it» or «Install» starts the download at once instead of asking a second time.

### New — from Prism
- **A missing add-on is offered on the spot.** When something needs one (a video link, Play with the in-app player, Extract, an AniList link), a small card names it, says why and how much it downloads, and «Get it» shows the progress in the same card, the way the app's updater does. Then what you asked for carries on.
- **Download details, as Prism draws them:**
  - The percentage large, with speed, time left and connections.
  - The file map, each connection's range in its own colour with a write-head.
  - Numbered connection lanes with their range and speed.
  - A smoothed speed chart of the last two minutes with its scale and a live dot, and Now · Average · Peak · Time left.
  - Info whose values copy with a tap, and Verify, Convert and Make smaller once done.
  - Active downloads show a small speed line in the list.
- **Status bar** on Downloads: total download and upload speed, active and queued, the next scheduled start, the speed limiter at a tap, and free space.
- **Sounds:** Prism's eleven «download complete» sounds to choose from, with a preview and volume, plus one for a failure and one for when everything has finished (Settings → Sounds).
- **«Download complete»:** a card with the file, how long it took and its average speed, Open, Folder, Copy path, Verify, Convert and Make smaller, and «Don't show again».
- **Antivirus (computer):** finished downloads of the kinds you choose are checked by Microsoft Defender, ClamAV or a command of your own, and the card says «no threats» or warns you.
- **Drag and drop** links, magnets or a .torrent onto the window. **Keyboard:** Ctrl/⌘ N adds, Ctrl/⌘ V adds what you copied, Ctrl/⌘ , opens Settings, Ctrl/⌘ L toggles the limiter. On Downloads, Ctrl/⌘ F searches, Ctrl/⌘ A selects all, Delete removes, Space pauses or resumes, Escape leaves the selection.
- **Video:** keep chapters in the file; download **only a part** (from–to); choose the file name's pattern; number a playlist's videos or not; on a computer, use a browser's sign-in for videos that need it.
