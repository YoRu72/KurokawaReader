### Fixes
- **PDF pages no longer go blank for seconds after the pages grid**: the grid's thumbnails were drawn through the same renderer as the pages and kept them waiting. Pages now come first; thumbnails still queued when the grid closes are dropped; a pinch on the grid no longer opens the page under the fingers.
- The dots and polka textures are gone (a page that had one is plain again).
- Dead code removed.

### Updates
- **Updates are fetched by themselves** (on by default; a switch in Settings → Updates). On Android, **Kurokawa Installer** — the Tools app's — takes the update when Tools is installed; the system's installer otherwise (your choice in the same place).

### From ReadEra
- **To read** shelf beside Favorites, from a book's sheet or a selection.
- **Font thickness** in the reading options (for fonts that carry weights).
- **Day / night** as a tap action for any of the nine tap zones (ReadEra's corner).
- **Web search engine** for a selected word: DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Wikipedia, Yandex or Baidu.
- **Leave the app only by pressing Back twice** (Android, off by default).
