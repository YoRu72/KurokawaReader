### Fixes
- **Covers that never appeared (OTL and other folders):** a card whose cover failed once was never tried again. Now it is retried after a site check passes, when AniList data arrives, and every so often while the shelf is open.
- **Covers getting slower and slower while scrolling:**
  - Covers are now made small by 2–3 background workers instead of one, and each picture is decoded straight at the size shown instead of at full size.
  - A slow answer no longer holds up the covers behind it.
  - A card that scrolls onto the screen moves to the front of the queue.
- **Covers on mobile data:** covers up to 1.5 MB are now read (the limit was 700 KB, and many store covers are bigger, so their series showed none). A series whose volumes have no cover at all shows its AniList cover.
- **Blank "Confirm you're human" window (novelbin and others):** the app was running its own hidden check on the same site at the same time, and the two broke each other. Opening the check window now stops the hidden one. The window also shows a loading bar.
- **Back button:** back inside a series opened in the library now closes the series instead of putting the app away. On PC, the mouse's back button and Alt+← do the same.
- **PDF → Word:**
  - Headings in the legacy "AL-Mohanad / AL-Mateen" fonts (common in Gulf books) now read correctly: «الإعراب»، «الشاهد فيه» instead of «الإعزاب»، «الظاِد فيْ».
  - Calligraphic titles that showed as "K" now use their own font.
  - Footnotes at the foot of a short page no longer jump to a page of their own.
- **Furigana and pinyin:**
  - Copying, searching inside the book, highlights and read-aloud now skip the readings. Before, they gave "漢字かんじ", lost highlights in annotated books, and read every word twice aloud.
  - Furigana now sits over each group of kanji, with the kana that follows outside: 取[と]り扱[あつか]い.
- Arabic wording: «ملفات تعريف الارتباط» replaces «كعكات».

### Faster and lighter
- **Startup:** the library loads about 0.2 MB of code instead of 1.2 MB. Only your interface language is loaded, and the reader, settings and the file-format readers load when first needed.
- **Fixed memory leaks:**
  - Leaving the library no longer leaves old copies of it in memory.
  - A closed book no longer stays in memory or keeps saving its place.
  - Library search no longer redraws on every key press.
- **Cover storage:** saved covers are now WebP (about a third smaller), slightly smaller in size, and capped at 40 MB in total. The ones you haven't seen in longest are removed first.
- About 1 MB of unused PDF engine files removed from every build.
- **Web app:** installing it for offline use downloads about 4 MB instead of 18 MB. Sample books, PDF engine files and extra fonts are saved the first time they are used.
- Big libraries: the list of books is kept in memory after the first read, so the library, sources and history no longer re-read every book each time.

### New
- **Secure DNS (DNS over HTTPS), like 1DM+:** in Settings → In-app browser, choose Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, AdGuard or your own resolver, then press Test. It covers the browser, the sources and the downloads, and helps when your network blocks a site by name.
- **Open the browser from Settings:** a full browser with an address/search bar, back and forward. No link needed.
- **"Solve it in the browser":** when a site's check window doesn't work, open the site in the app's full browser and pass the check there.
- **AniList sign-in links by itself:** the sign-in page opens inside the app and the account is linked when you approve it. No more copying the token.
- **OTL genres and tags from AniList:**
  - A series' page shows its genres and best tags. One tap shows every tag, grouped by kind (themes, cast, setting…). Spoiler tags stay behind their own button.
  - Tapping a tag finds similar series.
  - New filters: **Type (JP / KR / CN / Other)**, Genres and Tags (each can be included or excluded), **Match: All (AND) / Any (OR)**, and sort by AniList score.
- **New filter sheet** (like Mihon's and the manga sources'):
  - Reset and Filter buttons at the top, and filters in sections that fold open.
  - Tap a sort again to reverse it.
  - Tick a genre or tag once to include it, twice to exclude it.
  - Long lists have a search box and are split by first letter.
- **Bookmarks & notes:** each book's entries start collapsed. Tap the book to open them.
