### Fixes — PDF → Word, spaces between words
- **Text boxes lost every space.** On pages laid out as boxes (designed pages, framed text), the words of a line ran together: «متىتصبحمسافةالفرملةأطول؟», «فيصلبنعليالمنصور». The spaces the page draws are now kept inside each box.
- **Spaces lost in PDFs made by Word** where the space is drawn with no width: «كسرةالرويفي» is now «كسرة الروي في»; a quoted word no longer sticks to the word after it.
- **Wrong spaces inside words removed**: «كلم ة» is now «كلمة», «إبراهي م» is «إبراهيم», «ا لعربية» is «العربية»; and no space before «،».
- Dates whose parts the page sets apart are no longer joined and reordered as one number.

### Known, not yet fixed
- Lines drawn one glyph at a time with very tight word gaps (seen in one book's verse lines) can still come out joined.
- In one tested book, some numbered list labels in boxes now have a space after the number where they had a tab.
- Lines merged into one paragraph are the next piece of this work.
