### Anime
- **Choose studios from a list** in the filters, instead of typing a name: AniList's studios, the best liked first, narrowed as you type (accents and capitals ignored), with any studio AniList finds by that name added under them. Tick as many as you like; the ticked ones stay on top; show animation studios or producers alone; «Clear all» and «Done». The chosen studios sit in the filters as chips, each taken off by a tap.
