### Fixes
- **Direct links download again.**
  - Sites behind a protection page (DDoS-Guard, Cloudflare) are sent the cookies they set, including those from a check passed in the new browser.
  - The file gets the name the server gives (or the one in the link's `?f=`) instead of a code.
  - A web page sent instead of the file is reported as such, with a button to open the site in the browser, instead of being saved under the file's name.
  - With DNS over HTTPS on, a connection that fails on the way is tried once more with the network's DNS.
- **The app no longer closes by itself while downloading** (Android):
  - The background service could crash the app when it was stopped before it had started; that can no longer happen.
  - The service uses a kind with no daily time limit.
  - If the app does close unexpectedly, a card says so the next time it opens, with the report to copy or to send.
- **A torrent added by magnet no longer goes back to «Getting metadata» after the app restarts:** its details are kept with its progress.
- **The download list survives a write cut short:** it is read back from its last good copy, and one bad entry no longer loses the others.
- **Each tab remembers its screen.** Leave a show's page for Downloads and come back: the same page, scrolled where it was. Tapping the tab you are on still goes to its start.
- **Subtitle sources fixed:**
  - OpenSubtitles works without a key.
  - SubSource uses the site's open API (no key).
  - AnimeTosho finds the episode by its AniDB number and reads faster.
  - Podnapisi asks for all your languages at once.
  - A source that needs a key (SubDL, Jimaku) says so and asks for it on the spot; one behind a browser check opens the site in the app's own window, then searches again.
- **«Grab links from a page» finds what pages draw with their scripts:** open the page in the browser and tap Grab. You can then pick from everything found: by kind, «Other links», or all.

### New — from Hayase, Senpwai and Seanime
- **Tap an episode** to:
  - **Watch now:** the best release is found by your choices for the show, then played while it downloads, with a card showing where it is.
  - Watch it online through a streaming extension.
  - Download the best release, or choose one yourself.

  Episodes downloading show their progress bar and percentage as they go.
- **Search anime by tags and genres** (Hayase, Senpwai):
  - Tags can be required or left out.
  - Format, status, season, year, least score and sort.
  - Tapping a genre on a show's page searches by it.
  - With nothing typed, the search shows genres to browse by.
- **The anime library** (Seanime, Senpwai):
  - Your download folders, and any you add, are read for episodes.
  - The files are matched to their shows on AniList, by hand when a title doesn't match.
  - «Continue watching» plays the next episode from the disk.
  - Each show says how many episodes are on the disk and how many are missing.
  - Episodes on the disk play from their show's page, and are skipped when downloading a range.
- **Missed sequels** (Seanime): the shows you finished whose sequel you haven't started, under My list.
- **Review the picks before adding a range** (Senpwai):
  - Each episode's release is shown, and can be changed from the others that fit.
  - Unsure picks are marked.
  - When every pick is clear, they are added after a ten-second countdown, which you can stop.
- **Among equally good releases, prefer** the best quality, the smallest, or the most seeded (Hayase), in Settings → Anime.
- **Hide spoilers** (Hayase): episodes past the one you are on keep their titles and pictures hidden.
- **Fillers:** Hayase's filler list is asked first, then AnimeFillerList.
- **Torrents** (Senpwai), on Android:
  - Through the Connection proxy (peers and trackers too).
  - Kept to one network interface (a VPN's).
  - Anonymous mode.
- **Storage** (Senpwai): what your downloads take, by kind, and the free space, in Settings → Storage.
- **The app's browser:**
  - **+ → Browse the web** opens sites in the app (on a phone, with DNS over HTTPS when it is on).
  - A file a page downloads, or a magnet you tap, comes to the Add sheet with the site's cookies and address.
  - **Grab** brings back everything the page links to and the media its player loaded, to pick from.
- **Fonts for styled subtitles** (Player add-on): a fonts folder, or fonts added to the app. A subtitle that names a font gets it.
