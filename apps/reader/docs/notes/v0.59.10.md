### Fixes
- **Back no longer throws you out of the app** after a file picker or a lost sheet: at the library's top the first press says "Press Back again to leave"; a second press within two seconds leaves. (Settings can turn it off.)
- Long result lists in Browse scroll more lightly: cards far down the page are not laid out until they come near.

### المكتبة الشاملة
- **Text search as the site shows it**: hits inside the books are rows now — the book and its author, the page, and the passage with **the matched words lit** (the site's own marks, and the words of your search marked by the app, with or without «ال» and the clitics, harakat ignored). **Show more** brings the paragraph itself from the page. A tap opens the page.
