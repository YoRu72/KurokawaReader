### Voice subtitles: pick the model
- **Each model shows what it gives and what it needs**: accuracy and speed (out of five), the memory it needs and the size of its file, and whether it is English only. That way you can pull the best one your device can run.
- **Your own machine**: a speech-to-text server you run yourself, with the model you loaded. Speaches (faster-whisper), LocalAI, or any server with OpenAI's `/v1/audio/transcriptions` works, and so does **whisper.cpp's own server**. No key is needed. On a phone, enter your computer's address on the network.
- **Any other model** by its name, or a custom address. Nothing is downloaded by the app.
- Groq: Whisper large-v3 (the most accurate), large-v3-turbo (nearly as accurate, much faster) and distil-whisper (fastest, English only).

### Phone UI
- **Swipe a download**: to the right to pause it, resume it or retry it; to the left to remove it (you're asked first). The action shows under the card while you swipe, and a short swipe does nothing.
- **The bottom bar with many places** shows every place by its icon and names the open one, instead of cutting names to «Downloa…».
- **Settings** sections form one tighter list, in normal case instead of capitals.
