### Fixes
- **Anime — the pictures and the page that broke a few versions ago**: DNS over HTTPS could be on while Settings showed it off. The «Turn on» of the app's browser (its DNS sheet) switched it on in the phone's side and never told the Settings, and the Settings sent their «Off» to the phone only when they were not off. Since 0.14.10 every connection of the app's pages went through the app's DNS proxy while it was on — AniList's pictures and its answers among them — and a picture the page gave up on made the proxy force the site through a rewritten hello, which AniList's servers refuse. Now:
  - the Settings decide: their choice, «Off» included, is given to the phone at every start, and a switch turned in the browser is written back to them — **after updating, DNS over HTTPS is off unless you turn it on in Settings**;
  - with DNS over HTTPS on, a site the network gives as it is goes through the proxy untouched — its own address, its hello as the page wrote it — as before the proxy; a site refusing the rewritten hello goes back to the plain way for a day; one dropped connection no longer forces a site (two within minutes do);
  - a picture that fails to load is asked once more through the app's own way of reaching sites (the phone's client, then the page's fetch), and shown from what came.
