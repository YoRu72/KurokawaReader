### Fixes
- **The crash report now appears** (Android). When the app closed by itself while downloading, nothing was shown next time: the report was looked for only after a second and a half, and a crash inside the torrent engine (native code) was never written down at all. Now Android's own record of how the app ended — native crashes, «not responding» and memory included, with the readable lines of the trace — is written down the moment the app opens again, and the card shows at once. Copy it from the card and send it, so the cause can be fixed.
- **The page's renderer ending no longer closes the app.** When Android stops the page to free memory (or it crashes), the app reloads it and notes it in the report, instead of quitting.
- **Fewer ways for the engine to take the app down.** Errors of every kind in the torrent alerts, the progress ticker, the direct-download threads and the bridge calls are caught and shown as the task's error.
- **Long values on a download's details page** (addresses, paths, hashes) no longer push the page sideways: they are cut to three lines, and a tap shows them whole; on a phone each label sits above its value.
