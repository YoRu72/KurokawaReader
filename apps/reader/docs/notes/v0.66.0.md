
## Fixed

- **Book details on the phone:** the labels under the action tiles were cut short («Favo…», «Track…»). The tiles now sit in two rows when they do not fit in one, with their labels in full.

## New

- **Review a novel on AniList.** When your AniList account is linked and the book is matched to its entry, the details page and the tracking sheet offer *Review on AniList*: a summary, the review itself with a live count of characters, a score out of 100 and a *Private* switch. Your highlights can be inserted as quotes, and so can your reading facts. The draft is kept with the book as you type and goes into backups; nothing is sent until you press *Publish*. A review you have already published opens for editing, and can be deleted.
- When *Publish* is not yet available, a line above it says what is missing.

## Not checked

- Publishing was tested against a stand-in for AniList, not the site itself. If AniList's limits differ from those the sheet expects, its own message is shown and the draft is kept.
- The draft is not carried between devices by sync.
