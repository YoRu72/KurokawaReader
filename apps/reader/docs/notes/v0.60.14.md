### Fixes
- **Text as image on a PDF: the card is right now.** Lines are joined into running text (it used to show one ragged card line per PDF line), a word broken at a line end is put back together, and a selection across two pages comes out in order (the second page's words used to come first).
- Copying text selected across two PDF pages gives the words in order.

### Text as image for PDFs
- **"As on the page"** (new, the default on PDFs): the picture is the selected part of the real page, with its own fonts, harakat, colours and ornaments, drawn sharp from the file itself. Whole lines are kept with their marks above and below, the other column of a two-column page is left out, and a selection across two pages is stacked in order.
- **"As a card"**: the words set again as a card, as before. Your last choice is remembered.
- Options under the preview: the whole width of the text column or only the selected words, the title and page number under the picture, and the page as shown when dark pages are on.
- **"Cut a part of the page"** (page menu, after "Copy page image"): drag a rectangle on the page to save, share or copy just that part. This also works on PDFs whose text can't be selected. Scroll and zoom the page into place first.
