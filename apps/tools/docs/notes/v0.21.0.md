### Fixes
- **In-place unpacking is hardened.** The known limit of 0.20.5 is closed: job files are validated before use, restore never overwrites a file that is not the job's own, a job found beside an archive is finished only when this app started it, and an archive that was replaced beside a stopped job opens as itself. A blocked job can be forgotten (its records only).
- **The new computer build now has the unpacking shelf, undo and byte-for-byte restore** that only the previous build had.
- **Making a zip never replaces an existing file unasked.**
- **Outside players get subtitles again**, and sign-in headers are never placed on a command line.
- **A stream is released only when nothing will play it**, and the «watched?» question no longer holds it open.
- **The player's local addresses are signed per run**, and a page's cookies go only to the site they came from.
- **Arabic shows the digits 0-9** unless you choose otherwise; the Digits setting had no effect before.
- **Arabic wording and right-to-left layout** corrected across the player settings, archive questions and toasts: paths and names no longer flip, buttons in a row share a width.

### New: device battery (computer, Windows)
- **A page for the batteries of your devices:** game controllers, mice, keyboards, headsets, earbuds with each bud and the case, and a phone through Phone Link.
- **Low and full notices**, with a threshold per device.
- **Runs in the tray**, and can start hidden with the system (off until you switch it on).
- **Connect and disconnect Bluetooth devices from Tools**, and find and pair new ones. Pairing is confirmed in Windows' own window; the app never enters or keeps a PIN.
- **Safe by design:** the app only reads. Devices that must be asked before they answer are off until you allow them, and the app can send only a fixed list of known questions.
- **Not yet tried on real hardware.** Every reader is ported and tested against recorded examples only. A device may be missing or show a wrong level; please tell us which.

### New: fast file search (computer)
- **«Everywhere in my folders» in Files** (Ctrl+Shift+F): choose folders once, then find files by name as you type. The index follows changes, is kept in one checked file, and nothing leaves the computer.
- It can pause while the computer is on battery.

### New: watching
- **Choose how each kind of video plays:** the app's player, a player on this computer, or ask each time. The position comes back into your watched state.
- **Clear notices** for a file with no sound or a picture that cannot be decoded. Protected streams are refused up front.

### New: animated backgrounds
- **A scene engine with fourteen scenes** (snowfall, rain, aurora over a lake and more) that react to the pointer and keep text readable.
- **Mix your own:** a ground, a scene and up to two overlays with amount, size and wind; Snowy and Rainy presets; saved mixes.
- **Your own pictures and videos as backgrounds**, with fit, veil, blur and colours matched to the picture.
- **A browser for a Wallpaper Engine library** on this computer: its video wallpapers can be copied in.

### New: themes
- **Twelve new themes** and a real editor: two accents, tint, glass, roundness, spacing, size, and colours per background. Themes are plain files you can export and import.
- Yellow and orange are not used: a colour in that range moves to the nearest one allowed, and text that would be too faint is corrected on save.

### Not checked on real devices
- Battery readers, Bluetooth connect and pairing, the tray and the hidden start on Windows.
- File search on real drives with millions of files, and its live updates on Windows.
- MP4 (H.264) video backgrounds and a real Wallpaper Engine library.
