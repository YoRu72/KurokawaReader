### Fixes
- **Screenshots in the player really save on recent Android.** The frame goes to Pictures/Kurokawa through Android's own gallery store, and the message says «Saved» with the file's name and an «Open» button, or says what went wrong. Before, it said «saved» while Android had refused the file.
- **A tap hides the controls while paused**, and a tap shows them again. They still never leave by themselves while paused.

### New in the player
- **A play/pause button in the middle** of the picture, shown and hidden with the controls.
- **«Save frame» pad** from the camera button: previous and next frame (hold to repeat), one second back or forward, the exact time, PNG or JPG, and a «With subtitles» switch that is remembered. A long press on the camera, or the S key, saves at once with your choices.
- **«Make a clip from here»** in the pad opens the clip sheet starting at that frame.
- **Clip subtitles, three ways:** none, burnt into the picture, or kept as a track. Kept as a track saves MP4 (the words and timing; MP4 cannot hold styles or fonts) or MKV (the track as it is, with the fonts it uses attached). Picture-based subtitles can be kept only in MKV.

### Known limits
- Subtitles the app draws itself over the picture (not through the player engine) are not in a saved frame yet.
