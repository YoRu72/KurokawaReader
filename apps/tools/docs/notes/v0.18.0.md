### Fixes
- **Videos that need a sign-in can be signed in to, on a computer.** When a site asks you to sign in before it will give a video, choose *Sign in*: the app's browser opens on the site; sign in there, come back and choose *Use my sign-in*. The sites you have signed in to are listed in Settings → Video, each with *Remove*. Only that site's cookies are kept, in the app's own folder, readable by you alone.
- **On a phone, reading a video no longer waits without end.** It stops after a time and says so, with *Try again*.
- **Short links from X (t.co) work.** The link is followed once and the address it leads to is used.
- **An X post that cannot be read says why** — not found, private or protected, marked sensitive (reading it needs a sign-in), or the post readers did not answer — instead of the panel vanishing.
- **Errors in plain words, with something to do.** A video that cannot be read now tells you what happened (a sign-in is needed, a bot check, a limit on requests, not available in your country, removed, not live yet, an out-of-date yt-dlp…) and offers the action that fits: *Sign in*, *Try again*, *Open in the browser*, *Get deno*. The line yt-dlp itself gave is kept under *Details*.
- **A video site's page is no longer offered for download «as it is»** when the video could not be read. Download now waits until the video can be read, instead of saving the page as a file.
- **A playlist no longer starts every video at once.** A new setting, *Simultaneous video downloads* (Settings → Video): 2 on a phone, 3 on a computer; the others wait their turn, and each waiting video shows its place in the queue.
- **yt-dlp updates itself once and tries again** when a site's reader fails, and says it did.
- **GIFs from X are named as GIFs** («…-gif.mp4»), apart from a video of the same post.

### Faster
- **Paste a video link, get the format list sooner.** Measured against stand-ins for the sites, not the real ones: on a computer the app's own time went from 1.5 s to 0.8 s, and for an X post from 0.8 s to 0.2 s; on a phone from 3.4 s to 2.3 s, and less when the link is pasted a moment after the app opens. A download starts without reading the site a second time.
- **Nothing wakes the app when it is idle**, in front or behind.
- **The list under active downloads costs about a fifth** of what it did: only the rows that changed are redrawn.
- **A list of thousands shows in about a fifth of the time**, keeping only the rows near the screen.

### Changes to know
- **A link you copy inside the app's own window is no longer offered** as a download. Links copied anywhere else still are.
- **The empty-list animation plays twice** and then rests.
- **Progress bars in the list have square ends.**

### Known limits
- **On Android each yt-dlp run still starts its own Python** — about a second and a half on a mid-range phone, before the site is even asked.
