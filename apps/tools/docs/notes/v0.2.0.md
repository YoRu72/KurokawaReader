### Add-ons (Settings → Add-ons)
- **Anime** — an Anime tab: AniList sign-in (in the app, or a pasted token) and tracking like the reader's — status, progress, score, changes kept offline and sent later; the season chart with countdowns to each next episode (what AniChart shows) and the previous season's leftovers; the week's airing schedule (what LiveChart shows); your list, sorted by what is left to watch. Every episode is found as a torrent through the add-on's sources — Nyaa, Torrentio (Kitsu ids, your debrid key), SeaDex best releases, AnimeTosho — ranked by best release, confidence, your preferred quality and seeders, filtered to the episode; one tap downloads it in order so it can play while it comes, and the episode is marked watched when the download ends. **Extension repositories:** add a Hayase `index.json` or a Seanime marketplace or manifest in Settings → Anime and their anime torrent providers run inside the app, each with its own options.
- **Subtitles** — a Subtitles tab: a title, or an AniList / MyAnimeList / IMDb link, resolved into the show or film (AniList and IMDb cards, merged when they are the same work); pick season, episode and languages, and SubDL, OpenSubtitles, SubSource, Gestdown (Addic7ed), Podnapisi, Subf2m, AnimeTosho, Jimaku and YIFY are searched at once; results ranked by your language order; a tap downloads the file. Sites that need a free key have a field for it in their add-on settings.
- **Archives** — zip, 7z, rar, tar and gzip/xz/bzip2 opened from the add sheet or a finished download's menu: what is inside, extraction beside the archive, into a folder of its name or elsewhere, replace / skip / keep both, passwords, progress with cancel. **In place** for ZIPs: files come out from the end of the archive and the archive is cut back after each batch, so the disk needs room for the files only; a stopped run is continued later. Zips are made from files you pick. Subtitle archives are unpacked as they arrive (a setting), and every downloaded archive can be.

### Also
- Add-ons can declare settings (keys, choices); a gear on the add-on's row opens them.
- Add-ons reach the web through the app itself on Android and the desktop.
