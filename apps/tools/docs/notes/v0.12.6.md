### Fixes
- **Watch now lets you choose, as in Hayase.** It shows the releases from every source, the best one first, and the one you tap plays while it downloads. It no longer picks one by itself. To have it pick by itself again, turn on *Play the best release by itself next time* in that list. When the episode is already downloaded, or is in a batch you picked before, it plays from there straight away.
- **The watching card no longer covers the "Watch with" sheet.** The card goes away before the player asks, stays under every sheet, and keeps the title to two lines.
- **× on the watching card no longer stops the download.** It stops the watching only, and the download goes on (play it from Downloads), as in Hayase.
- **Fewer stops while streaming.** The part to download next now moves with playback all the time, about 48 MB ahead, even while what is playing is already there. Before, it was only asked for when playback reached a missing part, then waited for, so playback stopped at each gap.
- **Playback goes on in the background.** Video keeps playing when you leave the app or lock the phone; you can turn this off in the player's settings. The download goes on in the background with its notification, as before.

### New
- **Faster torrents:**
  - up to 150 peers per torrent (80 before; you can change it in Settings);
  - 500 connections in all;
  - more room for what peers send before it is written to storage, so a fast connection is not held back by the disk.
- **Installing an IPA is one tap after the first time.** The installer you used (SideStore, AltStore or TrollStore) is remembered, and *Install with …* opens it directly.
