### Fixes
- **No more crash while downloading on some Android phones.** The crash in the torrent engine's disk threads (seen on realme and HONOR phones) came from its memory-mapped file writing. Phones now use plain file writing by default. It is still a switch in Settings → Connection.
- **Paused downloads stay paused.** Downloads you paused no longer start again by themselves when you open the app.
- **The in-app player no longer shows a black picture** while the time moves. On Android, mpv now draws its picture in a way that shows under the controls on every phone.

### Downloads
- **Wi-Fi only** (Android): downloads wait while you're on mobile data and carry on when Wi-Fi is back.
- **Battery guard** (Android): downloads wait at 15% battery unless charging, and carry on when charging or at 20%.
- **Pause all from the notification**, and a **Resume all** notification while everything is paused.
- **Quick Settings tile** (Android) to pause or resume every download.
- **Slow mode**: one tap keeps downloads at a gentle speed, and a second tap gives back your old limits.
- **Share a finished file** to another app (Android).
- **Taskbar progress and a badge count** (PC/Mac), **keep the computer awake** while downloading, and an action **when everything is done**: sleep, shut down or quit.
- **Export and import the download list.**
- **A warning before adding a download you already have.**
- **Notes on a download.**
- **Retry all failed downloads.**
- **Download statistics.**
- **Clear finished downloads automatically** after a number of days you choose.
- **Open the page a download came from.**
- **Paste and download** straight from the clipboard.

### Player
- **Subtitles found by themselves**: when a video has none in your languages, the best match is fetched and loaded (needs the Subtitles add-on; turn it on in the player's settings).
- **"Ends at" time**: tap the time on the right to switch between time left, length, and the clock time the video will end.
- **Each video remembers its zoom, rotation, flips and crop.**
- **Sleep at the end of the video.**
- **A statistics overlay**: decoder, frame rate, dropped frames and buffer.
- **Keyboard shortcuts sheet** (press **?** on PC/Mac).
- **Copy the current time** with the video's name.

### Movies, TV and anime
- **Recent searches** for movies, TV and anime.
- **Rating filter** in Discover.
- **Watchlist and watching marks** on movie and TV cards.
- **IMDb button** and **copy the IMDb link** on a title's page.
- **"Surprise me"** in anime search opens a random popular show.

### Settings
- **Back up and restore your settings** to a file. Sign-in tokens and secrets are never put in the backup.
