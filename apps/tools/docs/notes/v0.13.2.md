### Fixes
- **Auto translation follows the page as you scroll.** Reading the screen as a picture (comics, sites that do not expose their text), the floating translator now finds how far the page moved, frame by frame from the picture itself. Every translation moves with its words at once, instead of disappearing and coming back after a new reading.
  - **Scrolling back is instant:** translations scrolled out of view are remembered where they are on the page, so they are there again the moment they come back.
  - Once the page stops, it is read again, and only lines not met before are translated.
  - Another page (a new chapter, another app) clears what was remembered.
- **With the accessibility service on,** the translations also move with a scroll the app reports, instead of hiding until the next reading.
- **Faster:** the screen is read again sooner after it stops (0.5 s by default), Google Translate is asked twice as many lines at once, and the translator's own views are hidden for a shorter moment when the screen is captured.
- **Better-fitting text:** a translation can now grow up to twice the size set to fill its words' box.

### New
- **A new menu for the bubble** (long press). At the top are chips for the translator (Google, DeepL or AI, tapped in turn) and the target language (a list). Below them, its actions are laid out as tiles: translate the screen or a region, the fixed region, auto on or off, how the screen is read, the list, copy, clear, smaller or bigger text, the app, and close.
