### New
- **A torrent up close** (Android): a Swarm card on a torrent's page.
  - It shows the seeds and peers connected and how many the swarm has, the peers sending and receiving now (their clients and progress), and every tracker with how it answered. It refreshes every two seconds.
  - **Check the files again** verifies what is on the disk.
  - **Find peers now** asks every tracker and the DHT at once.
  - Each download can have **its own speed limits**.
- **Watching while downloading starts sooner** (Seanime's order). The video's first 8 MB come first, then the next 24 MB, and its last 4 MB early too, where a video keeps the index a player reads to seek.
- **The next episode is fetched while you watch** (Seanime, Hayase). Halfway through an episode, the next one's best release starts downloading; from a batch, only its file. Turn it off in Anime settings.
- **Connection limits** (Hayase's): connections per torrent (80) and in all (500), in Torrent options.
- **Install an app** from the + menu: an APK, a bundle, or an archive with an app inside, picked from the phone and read where it is. Nothing is copied first.
- **Apps among a torrent's files** can be installed from the download's menu, one row for each app.
