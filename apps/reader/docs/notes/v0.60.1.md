### Fixes
- **Sites with an account — «Human verification is required»**: a site that asks a person to prove they are one at sign-in cannot be answered from the app's form. Its sign-in sheet now offers **Sign in on the site**: the site's own page opens in the app's browser, you sign in there (with whatever check the site asks for), close it, and the app takes up that session — the browser and the app share one cookie jar on the phone. A sign-in made in the app's browser at any other time is picked up the same way: the account shows signed in without asking for the form.
