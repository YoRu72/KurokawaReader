
## Fixed

- **The selection menu no longer sits on the selected text.** It is placed above the selection, or below it, never over its first or last line nor over the two handles, and it waits until you have finished moving a handle instead of jumping about. When a sheet such as *Text as image* opens, the selection is cleared, so its handles are no longer drawn on top of the sheet.
- **Library with three or more columns on the phone:** the format badge was cut off and ran into *Read*. On narrow cards the chips now fit inside the cover (*Read* becomes a check mark), titles get two full lines, and the stars stay inside the card.
- **Sliders are filled up to the thumb only.** Several sliders showed a stray filled block after the thumb.
- **Only the number of files** is shown under *Library*.
- The *Browse models* row in the AI settings was cut off.

## New

- **Titles and authors are yours to edit.** On a book's details page, *Edit* changes the title and the author, or adds an author where the book has none. A switch, on by default, keeps the old title as another title: a book may carry several titles, one is shown everywhere, and the rest are listed on its details page, where any of them can be chosen as the one shown. The original title and author are kept.
- **Search finds a book by any of its titles** and by its author, old or new. When the match is on a title that is not the one shown, the result says which.
- **Text as image: title and author on the card can be changed** before saving, or an author added. *Remember for this book* keeps the author, and *Use as the book's title* makes the new title the book's own.
- **The AI sheet has tabs:** Translation, Glossary, Names found and Saved translations sit side by side. Switch by tapping, swiping or with the keyboard; nothing needs closing first.
- **A glossary is a folder named after its book,** with one file for each kind of term found: characters, places, skills, citations and more, and files of your own. Each file can be exported by itself, or the whole folder at once. Renaming the book renames its folder.
- **A new volume can use the former volume's glossary.** When the titles match, the glossary is shared, copied or left empty, as you choose. The setting has three choices: *Automatically* (the default, applied only when the titles are the same apart from the volume number), *Ask me*, and *Never*.
- **Gemma for translation on the device, on the computer.** Gemma 2 2B is the recommended model, Gemma 3 1B a lighter one, and TranslateGemma works through a local server with its own prompt. On phones no on-device model is recommended for translation, since those small enough translate poorly: a computer or an online provider does better.
