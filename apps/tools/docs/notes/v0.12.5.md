### Fixes
- **Settings can always be reached on a phone.** With several add-ons (Anime, TV & Movies, Videos, Subtitles) the bottom bar had more tabs than a phone's width. Settings and the tabs after it were pushed off the screen. The tabs now share the width, and their labels shorten when needed.
- **Sign in to AniList from where you need it.** "Sign in to AniList" on a show's page and in My list now signs you in at once, instead of sending you to Settings.
- **The APK installer stays Android-only.** It is offered only in the Android app, as before; the new iOS installer below is offered only on an iPhone or iPad.

### New
- **An iOS app installer (IPA).** On an iPhone or iPad (Kurokawa Tools in Safari), *+ → Install an iOS app* opens an IPA from your files.
  - **Before installing**, it shows:
    - the app's icon and name, its bundle identifier, version and build;
    - the iOS it needs and the devices it's made for (iPhone, iPad);
    - its size installed, its extensions and frameworks;
    - what it asks to use (camera, photos…);
    - how it's signed: the provisioning profile's kind, team and expiry. An expired signature is flagged.
  - **Installing**: the app is handed to the installer you use: SideStore, AltStore or TrollStore. A link to an IPA opens in them directly; a file is shared to them.
  - **From a link**: a link to an IPA typed in *Add a download* is offered for installing.
  - **Downloaded IPAs**: an IPA you downloaded (or one inside an archive) can be looked into from its menu.
  - **Kurokawa source**: one tap adds it to SideStore or AltStore, so KurokawaReader can be installed and updated from there.
