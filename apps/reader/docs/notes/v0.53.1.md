### Fixes
- **Back didn't close the cover viewer:** on Android, back left the cover on the screen and went back in the app underneath. Back now closes the cover first, as it does a sheet or a menu.
- **Discover shows only with AniList linked:** the trending and popular light novels on the Browse page come from AniList, so they appear once you link your AniList account (Settings → AniList), not before.
