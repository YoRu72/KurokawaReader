### Fixes
- Sorting downloads by name now starts from A, and by time left from the soonest.
- The in-app player is reachable again from the Downloads list: its add-on was never listed, so finished videos opened in the browser's bare player.
- Selecting from the + button's menu on a phone works (the button could lose track of itself while its menu loaded).

### Smaller, by a long way
- **The app no longer carries its heavy parts.** On Android, the second ffmpeg (ffmpeg-kit) is gone, as yt-dlp's own full ffmpeg now also does Convert, Make smaller, clips and chapters. That ffmpeg's libraries and Python's are no longer inside the APK: they come with the add-ons that need them.
  - **Video** brings Python and ffmpeg: about 14 MB and 37 MB on a phone.
  - **Player** brings ffmpeg.
  - Each is downloaded once from this release, checked against its SHA-256, and listed in **Settings → Storage** with its size and **Remove**. Anything that needs one when it is missing asks first and says how big it is.
- **Computers:** ffmpeg left the installers and comes with the Player or Video add-on the same way; each installer keeps only its own system's 7-Zip.
- **Everywhere:** the TypeScript compiler for Seanime extensions (3.4 MB) is no longer built in. It is fetched once, pinned to its exact file, the first time a TypeScript extension is installed.

### New
- **Watch with:** Play asks whether to use the in-app player or another app (VLC, MX Player, mpv… on a phone; the system's player on a computer), and can remember the answer. Change it in **Settings → Player**. Choosing the in-app player the first time installs the Player add-on.
- **The + button opens a menu**, as in Prism: a link or magnet, what is on the clipboard, a torrent file, a video site, or every link on a page. The video-site entry says whether yt-dlp is ready (with its version) or where to get it. The Add sheet says so too when a video link is pasted, with **Add it** and a way to **Settings → Add-ons**.
- **Downloads options**, as the reader's Library sheet has them:
  - **Filter** by state, kind (torrents, direct links, videos, cloud), category and size, each with its count.
  - **Sort** by added, name, size, progress, speed, time left or state; tap again to reverse, and optionally keep active ones first.
  - **Display** as comfortable, compact or names only; group by state, category or day added; choose whether speed, time left, size and peers show.
- **Search** the downloads, and **select several** (the tick in the bar, or a long press) to pause, resume, copy their links, remove them, or remove them with their files.
