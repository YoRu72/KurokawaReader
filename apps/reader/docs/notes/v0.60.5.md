### Computer
- **Larger and easier on the eyes**: the interface is 10% larger on a computer to start with, and **Settings → Appearance → Interface size** sets it to 100–130%. An open book keeps its own text size, and PDF pages stay sharp.
- **A new sidebar**: the app's name, «Import books», each place with its icon and its name, Settings at the foot. Fold it down to icons with its button (remembered); a narrow window folds it by itself.
- **Settings in two columns**: the search and the sections in a column at the side, the settings beside them, instead of a row of chips a mouse couldn't scroll.
