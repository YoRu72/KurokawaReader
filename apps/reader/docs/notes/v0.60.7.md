### Reading on a computer
- **The tools in the top bar, with their names**: Contents, Search in book, Images, Add bookmark, Visual options and More, instead of a bubble of icons floating over the text. In a narrow window only the icons show.
- **Contents, bookmarks, notes and search beside the page**: they dock at the side, the text making room, and stay open while you read — jumping to a chapter or a search hit no longer closes them. Left open, the contents open again with the next book; close them and they stay closed.
- **Arrows to turn the page** at the sides of the text, shown while the mouse moves and gone when it rests. Space, Page Down and the arrow keys still work.
- **A picture opens with a double-click**, so a click while reading no longer opens it by accident.

### Computer
- **A book's page**: «Read» and the book's actions (favourite, reading time, review, share) sit beside the cover under its title, instead of a floating button and a row under the banner; the title is no longer repeated in the top bar; the chapters are a compact, numbered list in two columns.
- **The library's occasional tools in one menu**: search in books, convert, PDF tools, archives and a random book are under «More tools», each with its name; filters, choosing, downloads and Browse stay as buttons.
- **Settings like a computer's settings app**: the side list picks one section, shown open on its own and remembered; each setting is one line with its control at the end. Search still looks through every section.
