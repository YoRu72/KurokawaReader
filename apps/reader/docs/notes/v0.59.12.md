### Fixes
- **«Continue reading» inside a series** showed the library's last-read book, whatever it was; it now shows the last volume read of that series (as it already did inside a folder or a tag), or nothing.

### Text as image, with the picture
- **A picture beside the words you picked can go into the image.** When the words come right before a picture, it is included (a switch in the sheet turns it off); when they come right after one, the switch is there but off; when the picture sits between the words you picked, it is included and the words are laid out around it as on the page — the same when you picked the words above and the picture itself. The picture is drawn at the text's width with rounded corners, in both the plain and the card styles.
