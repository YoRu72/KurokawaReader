### Fixes
- **Finished videos wouldn't play on a computer** («The element has no supported sources», then «can't decode»): the player was given the file as `file://C:\…`, which isn't a valid address on Windows, and which a page served by the app may not read anyway. Files on the computer are now served to the player by the app's own local server, with seeking. «Open with the system's player» opens them by their path too.
- **When the file itself can't be read**, the player now says so (with the reason), instead of blaming the video's format.
- **«8192 TB free»**: free space was measured at a download folder that didn't exist yet and fell back to a meaningless number. It's measured where the folder will be, and shown only when known.
- **Pages without spacing**: cards on a page (the anime and TV pages among them) touched each other. They now have room between them, and a synopsis no longer shows big blank gaps.
- **A selected chip changed shape** (a pill among rounded rectangles): selected chips now keep the same shape and only change colour.

### The player is part of the app
- No add-on to install: the player is always there. Add-ons lists it as «Built in».

### Screen translator: fonts and a glossary
Real-time translation over the screen, comics in mind (Ascent's lettering, taken further):
- **Your own fonts**: add any .ttf or .otf file. Choose one font for everything, or **one for each kind of text**: speech balloons, thoughts, boxes (narration), sound effects, signs.
- **Each block's kind is told apart** by how it looks: text in parentheses is a thought, a dark or coloured box is narration, a few large letters with no balloon is a sound effect. Balloons and thoughts are centred.
- **Glossary**: names, places and attacks always translated your way, for one language or any. With an AI model the glossary is given to it; with Google, Microsoft and DeepL each term is masked from the translator and put back as yours. A term can also set the kind of block it appears in (`ドーン = بووم [sfx]` always in the sound-effect font).
- **Many terms at once**: paste them one per line (`original = translation`), and copy the whole glossary to keep or share.
- Works the same in the floating translator on Android and the screen translator on a computer.
