### Fixes
- **Torrent search was broken:** it asked every installed add-on, including the subtitle and anime sources, which answer a different question and failed. It now asks only the torrent-search sources, and Torrentio reads the newer format its streams come in.
- **Anime:**
  - Countdowns to the next episode now tick on their own (they froze at the time the page opened).
  - A season shows all its shows, not only the first fifty.
  - Typing fast in the anime search can no longer leave the results of an older query on screen.
  - Long series (One Piece and the like) list every episode, in pages of a hundred that open at your next episode; before, the list stopped at 500.
  - Episode titles and pictures from streaming services now match their episodes.
  - Shows on hiatus count their aired episodes from the schedule.
  - Removing a show from your list asks in the app's own sheet.
  - Arabic: times left read «يومان و٣ ساعات», «ساعة واحدة و٢٠ دقيقة» instead of abbreviations; counts of episodes to watch read «٣ حلقات للمشاهدة»; the season arrows point the right way.
- **Subtitles:** an anime episode is now looked up on the western sites by its season and episode there (episode 30 of a show is often S02E05 on IMDb), while the anime sites keep the anime's own number. Picking a title shows that it is working, and typing a name searches by itself after a moment.
- Results rows fill their width (some were pushed to one side).

### New
- **Torrent search, the OTL way:** what you type is looked up on AniList and IMDb — the works appear as covers, and the releases that come back are put under the work each belongs to, with its cover, year and IMDb id. Filter by quality, sort by seeders, size or name. Pick a work and the sources are asked again by its ids (a series by season and episode). An anime opens its own page when the Anime add-on is installed.
- **New search sources:** Knaben (one search across many trackers), The Pirate Bay, YTS (films) and EZTV (series by IMDb id). They are added to the Torrent search add-on automatically.
- **Add-ons update themselves:** a changed source is fetched again quietly, and a group gains its new sources.
- **Extension marketplace** (Settings → Anime → Browse extensions): every anime torrent source of the Seanime Marketplace (marked working, broken or retired, filtered by language), the Hayase extension repository, and more community lists — each installed with one tap. **Stremio add-ons** (Torrentio, Comet, MediaFusion, or any manifest address) work as anime sources too, answering by Kitsu or IMDb id.
- **Anime:** your list shows every status — watching, planning, paused, completed, dropped, rewatching. The episode sheet has quality chips and sorting, and a failed source tells why when tapped.
