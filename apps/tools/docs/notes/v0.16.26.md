### Subtitle packs
- **Give a show its subtitles once.** Point an anime or a TV series at a folder or an archive (.zip, .7z, .rar) of subtitle files whose names carry the episode numbers. From then on, each episode you play loads its own subtitle by itself, with no picking every time. It loads before any online search, and the online search doesn't replace it.
- **Set it up before you watch.** The anime's page (in the Episodes card) and the TV series' page have a **Subtitles** button. It shows the pack, which episodes it covers and which are missing, and which file goes with each episode.
- **Find subtitles from the show's page.** The same sheet searches the subtitle sources (when the Subtitles add-on is on) for one episode or a whole season. What you choose is saved into the show's own folder (`Subtitles/<Show>` in your download folder) and becomes its pack, or is added to it.
- **Wrong file picked? Change it.** The player's subtitles sheet shows the file chosen for this episode. "Choose another file" lists every file in the pack, the matching ones first, with a filter. Your choice is remembered for that episode and is never overridden. "Back to automatic" undoes it.
- **Shifted numbering.** If the file you pick shows the pack is numbered differently (say, file 17 for episode 5 of season 2), you're offered to apply the same shift to the other episodes.
- **Several versions of one episode** (languages, groups) appear as tracks. The one you switch to is remembered for the show.
- **Names understood**: `S02E05`, `2x05`, `Season 2 - 05`, `EP05`, `#05`, `[05]`, `05v2`, «الحلقة 05», Arabic-Indic digits. Resolutions, years, codecs and batch ranges (`01-12`) are not mistaken for episodes.
- Fonts in the pack (a `fonts` folder or loose font files) are used for its ASS subtitles.
- When you load a subtitle zip holding several episodes, the player offers to keep it as the show's pack.

Note: on Android, a pack folder must be on the phone's own storage (a folder on an SD card can't be read; use an archive instead).
