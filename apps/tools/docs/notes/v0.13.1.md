### Fixes
- **Downloads to a folder of your own no longer fail with «EPERM».** Android 11 and later let an app save only into its own folders, Download and Documents. A folder you made at the storage's root, such as *A K Tools*, failed with «open failed: EPERM». Now:
  - The app says so when you choose such a folder and offers two ways out: allow *All files access* (one switch in Android's settings), or keep saving into the app's folder in Download.
  - A download that already failed this way says «Folder not allowed». Its page has an *Allow the folder* button, and the download resumes once access is given.
- **Updates: choose the installer.** The update sheet now asks which installer to use: the app's own installer (progress shown in the app, then Android's confirmation) or Android's installer. Your choice is remembered. Before, Android's installer always opened.
- **The accessibility switch shows «Restricted setting».** Android 13 and later lock it for apps installed from a file. The Translate on screen page now explains how to unlock it: *App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings*, then turn the switch on. It also has a button that opens *App info*. Until then, reading the screen as a picture still works.

### New
- **Translate on screen in the Downloads top bar.** When the add-on is installed, its button sits next to the others at the top of Downloads. It is no longer in the + menu.
- **«Translate screen» as an app of its own.** On its page, *Show* puts *Translate screen* among your apps. From there you can add it to your phone's sidebar (Smart Sidebar, Edge panel) or the app drawer. *Home screen* pins it there. Opening it starts or closes the bubble without opening the app.
- **Open the browser without a link.** A globe button at the top of Downloads, and *Open the browser* in the Browse sheet, open the app's browser straight away.
