### Fixes
- **A long hold on a book only chooses it.** On some phones, letting go after the hold also opened the book. A long hold on a folder or a tag likewise only opens its menu now.
- **The snackbar rises straight up from the middle** of the screen instead of sliding in from the side, and in a book it sits above the reading bars, never over them.
- **Removing a highlight no longer leaves its text selected.**
- **Resetting the Visual Options keeps your background picture** until you are sure: its Undo brings everything back, the picture too.
- **Clearing the saved translations asks first** and says how many there are, since translating them again takes time (and, with a paid service, money).
- **Restoring a backup's settings asks first.** It says plainly that your current settings will be replaced. Books, notes and highlights are merged as before.

### New: interface styles
- **Choose how the app itself looks**: the library, the bars, the menus and the settings. *Settings → Appearance → App interface*. **Classic stays the default, and nothing changes until you choose a style.**
- **The styles:** *Paper & Ink* (soft paper grey with slate ink, and an ink-wash dark), *Midnight Stacks* (a library after hours: dark blue-grey glass), *Reading Room* (soft slate, dark and light), *Obsidian* (pure black, for OLED screens), and the three *Frosted Glass* styles by Wessam Lauf (Frosted Glass, Frosted Glass Light, Frosted Glass Dark). All are calm and easy on the eyes, and their text stays clear over any page or cover.
- **Pictures to download:** the Frosted Glass styles use background pictures (about 0.8 MB). They are downloaded only when you ask, with progress shown, and can be removed again.
- **Glass blur:** *Full*, *Reduced* or *Off*. Left to itself, the app picks what your device can afford and says why.
- **The app interface and the book page, side by side.** Appearance shows both together with a live preview. Choose either alone, or join them:
  - **«Use for the book page too»** gives your books the style's own page colours, once. It is saved as an ordinary theme of your own that you can adjust in Visual Options.
  - **«Keep the book page matched»** brings the matching page along each time you change style.
  - **«Keep the interface matched to the page»** works the other way round.
  - A book with its own reading theme keeps it, and the night theme still takes over in its hours.
- **Customise and save My styles.** *Customise* any style (the pencil on its card): colours, the glass, the background, your own picture, with a live preview, and Undo and Redo while you edit. Save it under your own name, then duplicate, rename, export or import it.
- *Settings → About → Licences* shows the notice of the styles that come from others.

### New: pinning
- **Pin a book, a folder or a series to the head of the list**, in the order you choose, whatever the sort.
  - **A book:** long-press it, then tap *Pin* in the bar (the second action).
  - **A folder or a series:** long-press it; *Pin* is the first entry in its menu.
  - **On a computer:** right-click, or press **P** on the selected card.
- **Pins belong to a place**: the library's main page, a folder, a tag or a saved shelf each have their own. A book can be pinned inside a folder but not on the main page.
- *Move pin earlier / later* changes the order. A search and a series' own volumes list as usual, without pins.
- Pins are kept in backups, follow a renamed tag, and go away when the book or folder is deleted. They stay on this device and are not synced.

### New: delete from the device too
- **Deleting books now opens the app's own dialog.** It says what goes with them (progress, bookmarks, notes, highlights) and offers **«Also delete the file from the device»** (on a computer: *from the computer*) when the original file outside the app can be deleted. The box starts unticked and is never remembered.
- **What can be deleted:**
  - **Android:** files in a folder you picked; files found by the device or Download scan, when the app has access to all files (Android 11 and later); a file opened from another app, only if that app allowed changes. These are deleted for good.
  - **iPhone and iPad:** files in a folder you picked in Files. These are deleted for good.
  - **Computer:** the original goes to the **Recycle Bin or the Trash**, so you can restore it from there.
  - **Web:** the app holds copies only, so there is nothing else to delete.
- The dialog counts how many files can be deleted, how many stay where they are and why, and any already gone. A file that could not be deleted is named in a message with *Show*, which lists where it lies.
- **Deleting without the box can be undone:** the books are hidden first and removed only once the *Undo* message has gone. Undo brings them back exactly as they were: progress, notes, folder, tags and pins.

### New: Undo and Redo everywhere
- **A message with *Undo*** appears after you remove a highlight, a note, a bookmark, a page note, a glossary term or folder, a text rule, a cover you set, a theme of your own, or pins. It also appears after you move books to a folder, delete books, apply a preset, reset the Visual Options, the buttons or the shortcuts, change a shortcut, clear translations, or restore a backup's settings. After *Undo*, a *Redo* is offered.
- **On a computer: Ctrl+Z** to undo and **Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y** to redo (⌘ on a Mac). These keys act on the screen you are on, and inside a text field they still undo your typing.
- **Visual Options keep a history** of every change while a book is open, with *Undo* and *Redo* at the top of the panel and of the reading options in Settings. A slider drag counts as one step. Choosing a style or a book page in *Appearance* is a step too.
- **The arrow beside each option** brings back the value it had before you changed it, then the default, then your own value again.
- **The ink eraser on a PDF can be undone**, and so can changes in the button editor.
