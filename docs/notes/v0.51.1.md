### Fixes
- **Downloads are much faster.** Books and app updates now download over up to 8 connections at once, in chunks, like a download manager. A server that limits each connection to about 500 KB/s now gives several times that. The progress line shows how many connections are in use.
- **More reliable downloads:**
  - a link that redirects from http to https works;
  - a server that doesn't report the file size, or ignores ranges, is downloaded in one piece instead of failing;
  - a connection that drops part-way is resumed from where it stopped, so no data is downloaded twice and progress never goes past 100%.
- **Updater:** closing the update window while the update downloads, then opening it again, no longer starts the installer twice.
- **Covers while browsing:** a volume's page (web folders such as OTL) now shows the cover read from its file, and tapping it opens the full-screen cover view. A series' cover opens the same way. Holding a book on a shelf offers «View the cover».
- **Cover view:** after you set your own cover, Share and Save give your picture, not the old one.
- **PDF → Word:** a damaged page can no longer leave a conversion waiting forever, and a page read a second time keeps its fonts.
- **Desktop:** the Alice sample book is fetched correctly when the app doesn't include it.
- Every screen was checked at phone and PC sizes, in English and Arabic, light and dark: no console errors, and nothing wider than the screen.

### Smaller apps
- The apps no longer ship source maps (11 of the 29 MB they used to carry).
- The Android, iOS and desktop apps no longer include the 4.4 MB Alice sample; «Add two books to start» downloads it when asked. Offline, only Maqāmāt is added.
- Desktop: Chromium's built-in language files now cover only the app's languages, and the installers use maximum compression.

### New
- **History** (like Mihon's): the clock in the library's top bar lists the books you read, newest first, grouped by day, with the time and your progress. Tap to continue reading, remove a book from the history, or clear the whole history.
