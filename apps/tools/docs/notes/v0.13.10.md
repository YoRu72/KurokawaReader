### Fixes
- **Sound for every video.** Many films, series and some anime have their sound in AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS or TrueHD, which the player's decoder cannot play, so they played silent. The player now finds each video's tracks with ffprobe. When the sound is one of those formats, ffmpeg turns it into AAC as it plays, and the app plays it beside the video, in step with it: playing, pausing, seeking and speed all follow the video. You will see «Sound (AC3) converted as it plays». Videos that ffprobe cannot read are still caught: a video that decodes no sound after a few seconds gets the same treatment.
- **Choose any audio track.** The sound menu lists every track the video has (language, format, channels), including ones the player cannot decode itself; picking one switches to it at once.
- **Streams start and keep going.**
  - On a phone, a seek no longer makes the download swing back and forth between the old place and the new one. The newest request now steers the download, and the old window's deadlines are dropped, so the download turns to where you are at once.
  - Requests the player gave up on are let go at once, instead of waiting up to two minutes each.
  - MKV files are now served the way the player expects (as WebM), on phones and computers alike. Some phones refused them before.
- **Clearer message for video the player cannot decode** (such as HEVC or 10-bit on some phones): it now suggests playing it with another app from the ⋮ menu.

### New
- **ffmpeg comes with the Player add-on.** Installing the Player add-on fetches ffmpeg with it, in one step. If you installed the Player before, you are offered ffmpeg once, shortly after opening the app. ffmpeg is used for the sound above, for clips and for chapters.
