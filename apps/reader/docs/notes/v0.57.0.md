### Fixes
- **The AI translator no longer stops at Gemini's «429 You exceeded your current quota»** on the free plan. That message is usually the per-minute limit (10 or 15 requests a minute on the free Flash models), not the day's. Now:
  - The app reads how long Gemini says to wait and waits that long. From then on it spaces requests to the limit Gemini names, so it no longer runs into it.
  - The wait shows on the translator's bar («Waiting for the provider's limit (37 s)…»), not as a red error, and translation picks up by itself.
  - For Gemini and Claude, each request carries more paragraphs (up to thirty), so a chapter takes fewer requests.
  - When it really is the day's quota, the translator says so in plain words and stops asking.

### New
- **Search a translated book in either language.** When a book is translated, wholly or in part, the search in the book offers Both · Original · the translation's language. Each hit shows the original above and its translation below, with the words found marked in either. Translations are now also kept by the original text, so they are found even after you change the model or the instructions.
