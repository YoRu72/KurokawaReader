### Fixes
- **A torrent's progress moves as the bytes come.** libtorrent counts a piece only once it is whole and checked. With many peers each filling a piece of its own, the count could sit at «0 B of 1.1 GB» while 1.3 MB/s came in. The blocks already received of the pieces under way now count too.
- **A torrent's map shows its pieces** (like Flud and qBittorrent), instead of the one-segment bar meant for links. It has a hundred stretches, each as solid as what of it is here, plus the count of pieces here.

### New
- **Faster torrents:**
  - Ten seconds' worth of requests is kept with each peer instead of three, so fast or distant peers are never left idle.
  - Pieces next to each other are taken together, so the disk writes in long runs.
  - Fast peers are asked for whole pieces, so pieces finish instead of all being half-filled.
  - A peer that drops is tried again sooner, and dead peers are let go sooner.
  - Twice as many upload slots, so more peers send back.
- **Videos** (with the Player add-on), REX Player's library, in a tab of its own:
  - **On this phone:** your videos by folder, with thumbnails, length, quality and how far you got. Sort and search across them. Hold one to play it from the start, mark it watched, open it in another player, or see its details. The app asks for Android's video permission first.
  - **Recent:** what you played, to carry on where you left off.
  - **Servers:** **Jellyfin** (sign in, browse your libraries with their pictures and progress, and play straight from the server) and **WebDAV** (a NAS or cloud folder, with its password used to sign each request).
  - **Open a video file** from anywhere.
  - Turn the tab off in Settings → Player.
- **Cutting a clip, REX Player's way:**
  - Drag A and B along the player's seek bar.
  - In the clip sheet, drag the range on its own strip, with its length shown, or nudge each end by a second or a frame.
  - Set an end to the player's current time, or show its frame.
  - Play the range on a loop.
  - It makes a video (fast or exact, with or without sound, subtitles burnt in), **its sound alone** (M4A), or **a GIF** (width and frame rate chosen).
  - Once done, open the clip from the sheet.
  - Hold «A/B» to open the clip sheet straight away.
- **Your own buttons on the player's bar** (REX Player's custom buttons): seek by any amount, switch to a speed, jump to a time, A/B, screenshot, next subtitle or audio track, next subtitle line. Add them under ⋮ → Your own buttons.
