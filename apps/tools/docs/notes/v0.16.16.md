### Fixes
- **An X post downloaded as an empty file**: with a post's link in «New download», yt-dlp said it was ready but offered no quality to pick, and «Download» made a file of 0 B that showed as done. A post is now read by the app's own reader (below), and a yt-dlp download that ends without a file — or with an empty one — fails with that reason instead of «done» at 0 B.
- **«Pick a quality below» with nothing below**: when yt-dlp reads a page but lists no qualities, it says Download takes the best it offers.

### Auto-grab
- **A post's photos and videos right in «New download»**: paste a link to an X (Twitter) post — or Bluesky, Reddit, Telegram, and on a computer the galleries gallery-dl knows — and its media are listed at once, each with its picture and kind, all ticked, at full quality. Untick what you don't want; «Download» (it says how many) adds the rest. No more «Show them» and a second sheet. It can be turned off in Settings → Downloads.

### Video sites
- **Why a page can't be read**: when yt-dlp can't read a video page, its own reason is shown. When the site wants a sign-in — Instagram, a private post, a YouTube «not a bot» check — «Use my sign-in for this site» opens the app's browser so you can sign in there, and the page is read again with that sign-in. Choosing a saved sign-in below the link reads it again too.
