### Fixes (PDF → Word)
- **Pages no longer run over.** A 27-page Arabic book came out as 38 pages, because justified lines were a little too wide and their last word wrapped. Such lines now get narrower spaces, and the book comes out as 27 pages.
- **Ruled tables are real Word tables again.** They were broken into loose text boxes, with header words landing in the wrong cells. Each word is now in its own cell.
- **Coloured harakat are kept.** Vowel marks drawn in another colour (red marks on black letters) came out black or went missing. They now show in their own colour.
- A ligature the PDF draws and then steps back over (such as a final «هي») no longer leaves a wide gap or puts words out of order.
- A cell that spans rows is marked correctly (only where the next row continues it).
- Left-to-right tables in a right-to-left document are no longer mirrored.

Still imperfect: text inside table cells can sit 2–3 points low, and a few words whose PDF text data is wrong can lose a letter.
