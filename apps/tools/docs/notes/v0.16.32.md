### Fixes (Files)
- **The phone's Back walks up folders.** It went straight to the main page. Now it closes what is open, leaves a selection, then goes up one folder at a time — out of an archive to the folder that holds it — and only from the top of Files returns to the page you came from. Each folder comes back scrolled where you left it. On the computer, Back and Forward walk folders too.
- **Copying out of an archive works.** Copy the entries, go to a folder and paste: they are taken out there, with progress and Cancel. Before, this failed with a red message, or put the files under their full inner path and replaced files of the same name without asking.
- **Inside an archive, what cannot be done is no longer offered.** Rename, delete and new folder are hidden; Cut is shown greyed and points to Copy. Open, Open with, Share, Copy, Extract here and Extract to… all work.
- **An archive's title is its name**, not a long encoded path, and the path line shows where you are inside it.
- **Each entry in an archive shows its date.** The line ended in a stray « · ». In Arabic the size and date no longer run into each other.
- **Long names keep their ending visible** (`HOREMAJO_03_KARAOKE_… D (1).ZIP`), in the list and the grid; a name in another script no longer has its brackets and dots thrown about.
- **"Keep both" names the copy "name (2)"**, not "name (1)".
- When several items are pasted and some fail, every failure is listed at the end; a cancelled job says so.

### New (Files)
- **A clipboard bar.** After Copy or Cut a bar stays while you browse: what is held (the name, or «العدد: {n}»), **Paste here**, and a close button. A copy stays for pasting again until you close it; a move clears when done. Where pasting is not possible — inside an archive, a folder into itself, a read-only place — the button is greyed and says why.
- Computer: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V in Files; Esc leaves the selection, then clears the clipboard.

### Changed
- The setting "Back goes up a folder" is gone: it is now how Back works.

### Known
- The computer app cannot copy, move or delete files yet, so Copy, Cut and the bar are not shown there; it comes with the computer app's rebuild.
