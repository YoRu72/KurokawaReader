### Fixes
- **Extensions stopped after 7–8 novels:** a site that answered the next page with an error (busy, too many requests) made the list look finished. Such a page is now asked again, and if it keeps failing the error shows at the bottom of the list with a **Try again** button. The next page loads a screen and a half ahead, with a spinner at the bottom, and a site that repeats the same novels shows each one only once.
- **"The site refuses the app" when LNReader works:** the app's browser took a site's own page (one saying you can't access a locked chapter, say) for a refusal. Now only a real Cloudflare or DDoS-Guard block page counts, and the app always tries the plain way once, as LNReader does, before saying the site refuses it.
- **Tapping a chapter opened the last chapter read:** tapping chapter 100 on a novel's page opened chapter 2 (the one opened before), and some extensions' chapters only opened from the contents inside the book. The chapter is now found in the book by its address (the book's list is brought up to date first when the site has new chapters), and the place asked for is kept while the book finishes opening.
- **Chapters cut short:** a chapter the site keeps inside a form (as ASP.NET sites do), a `<template>`, a `<noscript>` or an `<svg>` picture no longer loses everything inside it. Mangayomi extensions' quoted chapter text is unquoted as Mangayomi does, and a chapter's HTML line breaks no longer show as long empty gaps.
- **OTL EPUBs downloaded broken** ("Can't find end of central directory"): every download is now checked before it goes into the library. A file cut short, or a web page sent in its place, is fetched again the browser's way; if it's still wrong, the download fails with the reason instead of adding a broken book. A book that came broken before says so and has a **Download again** button that fetches it into the same book, keeping your place and notes.
- **Download all on a series:** it stopped at the first volume that failed, and after one volume when "device only" was chosen. Every volume is now queued at once (three download together), a failure doesn't stop the others, and the ones that didn't arrive are named at the end.
- **A novel or download added to the library didn't show until you opened it:** the library now updates as soon as a book is added, from the Browse page, a finished download or another tab.
- **Searching a whole novel:** the library's search across books now includes novels from extensions (their downloaded chapters). Searching inside a novel says how many chapters aren't downloaded yet and can download them, then searches again.
- **Pictures in EPUBs showed a blank or broken box while loading:** they now load before they reach the screen, and a picture that can't be shown is hidden instead of showing a broken icon.
- **Text could be selected on the app's own screens** (the library's "10 files · …" line, titles, buttons). Only what you read stays selectable: books, descriptions, notes, definitions and text fields.

### Faster and lighter
- Browse covers load before they scroll into view, not once they're on the screen.

### New
- **Zoom into pictures:** tap a picture in a book to open it, then pinch, double-tap or use the mouse wheel to zoom, and swipe for the next one. Turn it off in the reading options ("Tap a picture to open it and zoom in").
- **Download progress on library cards:** a book being downloaded shows in the library dimmed, with its progress, and clears when it's done. A novel whose chapters are being fetched shows the same way.
- **Pictures in a novel's chapters:** "Pictures in its chapters" on a novel's page (or Images in the reader) shows every picture across its chapters, and can download the remaining chapters to find all of them.
- **OTL synopses from AniList:** a series in OTL shows its synopsis from AniList.
