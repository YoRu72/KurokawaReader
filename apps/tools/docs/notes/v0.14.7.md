### Fixes
- **Back no longer throws you out of the app**: when nothing is left to close (after a file picker, say), the first press says "Press Back again to leave" and only a second press within two seconds puts the app away.

### Browser
- **DNS over HTTPS, visible**: a DoH chip on the browser's bar — grey when off, amber while a name is being asked, green once the resolver answered, red when it failed. Tap it for the full picture: the resolver, the last answer and how long it took, how many names were asked, how many sites came through DoH, whether this page's site is hidden by your network, and the last events — plus Test, and Turn on/off right there.
- **A browser menu**: open in the system browser, copy or share the link, desktop site, find in page, clear this site's cookies, DoH, home.

### Settings → Privacy
- **A live DoH status card**: state light, counters, the last events as they happen, and a Test button that tries the resolver and reports which address answered and in how long. On PC/Mac the test goes through the app's own secure resolver.
