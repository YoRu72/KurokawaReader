### Fixes
- **Files sent you to Search**: the bottom bar knew every tab but Files (and Courses), so in Files it lit Search, and Search's remembered place became the Files folder — tapping Search took you back into Files, and the Files tab lost its place. Every place now has its tab, and a new one can't be left without one.
- **The Files drawer did not open with a swipe on a phone**: once the screen took the finger for scrolling it stopped the swipe. It now follows the finger itself; swipe from the side to open, swipe back to close.
- **Files' kinds wrapped onto a second line**, and an empty folder's «Nothing here» was squeezed into a narrow column. The kinds are one line that scrolls sideways, and the message spans the list.
- **The phone's storage was called «0»**: it is «Internal storage» now.

### Files
- **Look inside an APK**: the install sheet has «Browse contents» (for a single app, a bundle, a locked archive, or a file that turns out not to be an app), and in an installed app's folder its base.apk and parts open as folders instead of the installer.

### Anime
- **Studios in the filters**: the best known ones to tap, any other found by its name, together with genres, tags, format, season and the rest.
- **Each episode with its picture**, 16:9 with its number over it: from the streaming sites, else from ani.zip (TheTVDB's pictures, as Seanime and Hayase use), with ani.zip's episode titles where the sites have none.

### TV & Movies
- **Each episode with its picture**: Cinemeta's, else TVmaze's for the episodes Cinemeta has none for.
