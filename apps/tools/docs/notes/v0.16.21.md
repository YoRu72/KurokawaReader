### Fixes
- **Add-ons: «Cancelled» when adding one back.** A download that failed for any reason (a dropped link, a server answering «canceled») was reported as if you had pressed Cancel, with nothing to retry. Now only your own Cancel counts as cancelled; any other failure shows its reason and **Try again**.
- **Downloads on a weak phone link** (seen on phones such as Tecno's): a connection that kept dropping every few hundred kilobytes used up its retries and failed the whole download near the end. A retry that brought bytes no longer counts as a failure, so the download keeps going from where it stopped until it is done.
- **Files**: at the top of a place, the path no longer repeats the title above it, and the grid's dates are short (one line, not two).
- **The bottom bar no longer sits on top of the keyboard** while you type (searching anime, for example).

### Faster downloads
- **Add-ons download over several connections at once**, like ordinary downloads. If that fails, they fall back to one connection.
- **Speed test** (Settings → Downloads → Connections per download): your phone's link is measured with 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 connections through the app's own downloader. A weak link is often faster with fewer connections, a fast one with more. The best number is offered, and every file download then uses it.

### Anime search finds what you're typing
- **Results while you type**: «Grot» now finds *Grotesqque*. AniList only matches whole words, so when it finds little, MyAnimeList's search (which matches the start of words) fills in, with the shows shown as usual.
- The previous results stay on screen, faded, while the next ones load, instead of flashing a spinner.

### Subtitles from the voice (AI)
- **For a video with no subtitles**: Subtitles → **From the voice (AI)**. The sound is taken out with ffmpeg and sent ten minutes at a time to a speech-to-text model (Whisper), and the lines it hears become a subtitle track on the video's clock. Only the sound is sent.
- **Services**: Groq's Whisper (fast, with a free tier), OpenAI's, or any address of the same kind. Add your key there once.
- **Translated into your language** if you choose one, with the player's translator, like any other subtitle.
- **Saved as ASS** (one clean style, Arabic in Uthman Taha Naskh, ready for typesetting) **or SRT**.
- A model that runs on the device itself, downloaded like ffmpeg, is not included yet.

### Buttons where you want them
- **Player**: Tools → Layout puts each button on the **bottom bar, the top bar, or neither**, in the order you choose. Every tool stays in the Tools grid either way.
- **Bottom bar**: Settings → The app → **Bottom bar** chooses which places it shows and their order. Settings always stays, at the end.
- **The + menu**: **Edit this menu** (at its end) leaves out the ways of adding you don't use, and orders the rest.

### Player
- **Arabic subtitles in Uthman Taha Naskh** when the video brings no fonts of its own: plain subtitles, and styled (ASS) ones whose fonts aren't in the video or the torrent. Other languages keep the regular font, and your own subtitle font, if you chose one, still comes first.

### Screen translator (Ascent's way)
- **The original words are taken off the picture** and the translation is lettered on it: a plain balloon stays plain, a gradient or sky stays as it was, with no box of one colour on top. This is the new default. «A box of one colour» and «Bare text» are still there (Style → Background).
- **Translate from and into, from the bubble**: the bubble's menu now has the source language too («Detect» first) next to the target.
- **English is the default target** for new setups.
