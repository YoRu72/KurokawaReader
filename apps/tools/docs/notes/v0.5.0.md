### Fixes
- **The desktop app opens again.** Versions 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 left a file out of the Windows, macOS and Linux builds, so the app stopped as it started. The build now checks that every file the app needs is packaged.
- Seanime extensions written in TypeScript load properly: they are compiled with the TypeScript compiler instead of having their types cut out by pattern, which broke most of them (2 of 17 community extensions loaded before, all 17 now).
- Seanime's `Doc` and `LoadDoc` behave as Seanime's own: `attr()` gives the value itself, and the rest of the selection methods are there (`siblings`, `nextAll`, `parents`, `data`, `attrs`, `end`, filters by function, …).
- «1 sources installed» now reads «Installed 1 source» (and is counted properly in every language).
- Episode numbers on a show's page use the same digits as the episode titles.

### New
- **Watch anime online.** Seanime's online-streaming extensions install from the same marketplaces (marked «Streaming», with a filter for them). A show's episodes get a ▶ button: the show is found on the extension's site (matched by its titles, season and year; **Change** picks another, and the choice is kept), the episode located, and the server asked for its video, falling back to the next server when one is down. Each quality has **Play**, in the app's player with the episode's subtitles, marking it watched near the end and skipping openings as for downloads, and **Download** (a file goes to the downloader, an HLS stream to yt-dlp, named after the show and episode). Sub or dub is chosen when the extension offers both. The headers a site requires are sent through a small server inside the app, and on the desktop HLS is turned into MP4 as it plays. Extensions that need a headless browser (ChromeDP) cannot run here and say so.
- **Season guide:** «For you» orders the season by your taste (learned from your AniList scores and statuses, each card showing how well it fits); the season's top ten in S/A/B/C tiers; **+ Plan** on shows not on your list.
- **Anime diary:** every episode you logged on AniList as a calendar with each day's shows, the shows you left unfinished and how far behind you are, and the year in review (episodes, hours, shows, longest streak, busiest day, months, top shows, genres, studios).
- AniList and MyAnimeList anime links opened with the app go straight to the show.
- **Privacy, from the reader:** DNS over HTTPS for the app's requests and downloads, so sites a network blocks by name are still reached; screenshots blocked; a choice of Arabic digits.
- **Settings backup:** settings, add-on settings and lists saved to a file and restored from it.
- **Torrent options, Senpwai's:** stop seeding at a share ratio or after a time; encryption required, preferred or off; DHT, local peer discovery, UPnP and NAT-PMP each on or off.
