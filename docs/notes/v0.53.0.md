### Fixes
- **Broken chapters:** a chapter the site gave as a "Just a moment…" / "Verify you are human" page, or as nothing, is no longer saved as the chapter. One saved that way before is fetched again the next time the novel is opened or downloaded.
- **AniList progress made offline is no longer lost:** a change made while AniList can't be reached is saved on the book and sent when the network comes back.

### New
- **Remove or replace text everywhere:** select words in a book (a site's watermark like "Read at novelbin…", a name you want spelt your way), then **More → Remove or replace everywhere**. A rule can apply to one book or every book, and can be a regular expression. Read-aloud and search follow the rules; the books themselves are not changed. Manage the rules in Settings → Text rules.
- **Open a shared link:** share a novel's page, a Shamela book or an archive.org item from your browser to the app (Android), or paste the link into "Search all sources" or "Import from link", and it opens in the source that has it. A link no source knows opens in the app's browser; shared words without a link are searched in all sources.
- **Duplicate warning:** a novel's page says when the same series is already in your library from another source, with a button to open it, and asks before adding it again.
- **Incognito mode:** switch it on at the top of the History. What you read then is not added to the history or the reading stats and is not sent to AniList. A dot on the History button shows it is on.
- **"Browser" button in the "Confirm you're human" window (Android):** when the check won't pass there, one tap opens the site in the app's full browser, with its address bar and back/forward, to pass it the usual way. Covers waiting on that site are tried again afterwards.
