### New
- **One-tap AniList sign-in, as in Hayase.** *Sign in with AniList* opens AniList inside the app: log in, tap Authorize, and the account is linked. The apps now have their own AniList client, so there is no ID to look for and nothing to copy. The guide for using a client of your own is still there, behind *Use an API client of your own*.
