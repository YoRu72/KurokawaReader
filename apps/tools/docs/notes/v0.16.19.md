### Fixes
- **Subtitles placed outside the picture, too big, in the wrong font**: on a phone held upright, subtitles sat in the black area under the video and over the controls. A CSS mistake (a percentage multiplied by pixels) also made the browser drop the subtitle font altogether, so lines came out in a large generic font. Subtitles now sit inside the picture, are sized from it (about a nineteenth of its height) and keep their own font.
- **Styled subtitles ignored the release's fonts**: the fonts a Matroska carries for its subtitles (its attachments), and font files in the torrent (a «Fonts» folder), are now handed to the subtitle renderer.
- **Fast clips started seconds late**: a fast cut copies the video as it is, so it can only begin on a keyframe. It began at A itself, and many players showed nothing until the next keyframe. It now begins exactly on the keyframe at or just before A, and says where («it starts at 1:28.4»).
- **Re-encoded clips failed on phones** (`frame=0 … Conversion failed!`): the phone's own encoder is now given the picture in the form most phones take (NV12). If it still refuses, x264 is tried, then ffmpeg's built-in MPEG-4. A failure shows ffmpeg's actual reason, not its last line.
- **A subtitle file read as a torrent**: a file opened with the app that wasn't a torrent (a file manager can label an .ass file as «octet-stream») was put into New download as a long block of text and failed with «Can't decode data». Only real torrents are read as torrents now; other files go to Files to be saved. A torrent handed to the app shows as its name, not its bytes.

### Watching torrents on a computer
- **Skipping ahead works while it downloads**: aria2 fetches a torrent's pieces in its own order and can't jump to where you skip, so skipping ahead could stall for minutes. While the player is open, the torrent now plays through WebTorrent (Hayase's engine), which fetches the spot you jump to first. In our tests, a jump to 80% of a file was served in under a second, where downloading in order took about 17 seconds. When you close the player, aria2 takes the download back and re-checks what's there, so nothing fetched while watching is fetched again.

### Clips
- **Cut in the background**: after «Export» the sheet closes and the clip is cut in the background, even if you leave the video. A card shows its progress, on a phone the percentage is also in the notifications, and a pop-up says when it's done, with «Open».
- **Saved in Movies/KUROKAWACLIPS** on a phone, where the gallery and other apps find them; on a computer, in the download folder.

### Player
- **Says why it's waiting**: when a torrent video stops to buffer, the spinner shows the download speed and number of peers, or that no peers are connected yet.
- **The torrent's own subtitles while it downloads**: subtitle files in the torrent that belong to the video playing (same name, or a «Subs» folder) are found and offered before the torrent finishes.
- **A steadier buffer on phones**: when watching a torrent while it downloads, the phone now keeps about 30 seconds of the video ahead, sized from its bitrate, instead of a fixed 48 MB.

### TV & Movies
- **Better automatic picks**: a camera copy, telesync or screener is never picked while an honest release exists, and a release with only one or two seeders (one leaving would stall playback) gives way to a better-seeded one.
