### Fixes
- Countdowns read «1 d» instead of «1 d 0 h» when no hours are left.
- An archive whose files would land outside the chosen folder, or that lists the same file twice, is refused before anything is touched; a file cut short by a cancelled extraction no longer stays behind.
- Player times keep one set of digits in Arabic.

### New
- **Archives, all of HatchZip:**
  - A **space plan** before anything changes — free space, what a normal extraction needs, what in place needs (its largest file plus a margin that is never used: 2 GB on a computer, 512 MB on a phone; set it in Settings → Archives), and what is left after. When only in place fits it is chosen for you; when nothing fits, nothing starts.
  - **tar** extracts in place too, besides ZIP; **Give up and restore** turns a half-extracted archive back into a normal one holding exactly what was not yet extracted.
  - **Test** reads every file without writing anything; **Verify first** does it before an archive is cut; an `.sfv` beside it is checked first.
  - **Extract here** puts a single item beside the archive and several into a folder of its name; files already there are counted and kept side by side by default; **several archives** can be picked and are extracted one after another.
- **App installer (Android):** a downloaded APK, or a split bundle (.apks, .xapk, .apkm, or a zip of APKs), shows the app's icon, name, package and version — new, an update of the one installed, the same, or older — then installs straight from the file with its progress (a bundle's parts are streamed from it, nothing unpacked), says why if Android refuses, can delete the file afterwards, and opens the app.
- **Anime, the Senpwai and Seanime way:** **Download episodes** fetches a range in one go with your choices (resolution, release group, subbed or dubbed, words to require or avoid), leaves out fillers (from AnimeFillerList), skips what you already have, and takes a batch when one covers them all. **New episodes** keeps those choices and fetches each new episode as it airs (checked when the app opens and every half hour). The show page marks fillers and downloaded episodes and plays the downloaded ones.
- **Player, REX Player's extras:** openings, endings and recaps skipped — a button, or by themselves — from AniSkip for anime and from a file's chapters; a +85 s skip chip; chapters marked on the bar and listed; picture filters (brightness, contrast, saturation, hue); an ambient glow; the time left at a tap; subtitles dragged up or down; the width of the double-tap zones; shuffle; open in another player (VLC, MX, mpv) on Android.
- **Downloader, Prism's tools:** **Convert** a video, song or picture (MP4, MKV, WebM, GIF, MP3, M4A, FLAC, Opus, WAV, JPG, PNG, WebP) and **Make smaller** to fit 8, 10, 16, 25, 50 or 100 MB, beside the original; **Verify** a file's MD5, SHA-1 and SHA-256 against a pasted checksum; **Grab links from a page**, sorted by kind, added together; a ZIP's contents shown before it is downloaded; the **clipboard monitor** offers a copied link when you come back to the app; the **scheduler** runs downloads added «later» in the hours and days you set; HLS and DASH stream links go to yt-dlp.
- READMEs in Japanese, Chinese and Korean beside English and Arabic.
