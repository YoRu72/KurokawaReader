### Fixes — book formats
- **Arabic titles of text files no longer garbled.** The title of a .md or .html file in Arabic (or Japanese, Korean, Chinese) often came out as meaningless symbols, and a .txt file was named after its file instead of its first line. Both are read correctly now.
- **Word (DOCX) numbered lists** are numbered again — they were all shown as bullets — with their levels nested, their own number style (1, ١, أ, a, i…), and inside tables too.
- **EPUB contents entries and footnote links that did nothing** now jump to their place. (The empty marks they point at were being removed; common in converted Arabic books.)
- **EPUB links with Arabic or encoded names** go to the exact place, not the chapter's start; and a book holding a file with «%» in its name opens.
- **Password-protected PDFs open on the computer.** The password is asked in the app's own dialog; a wrong one asks again.
- **Tapping a chapter on the book's page** opens that chapter, not the next one, in books whose contents list has broken entries.
