### Fixes
- **Two progress bars while ffmpeg or yt-dlp downloaded, with no way to hide them**: the download card now has one bar for the whole download, each part saying how far it is in words. While it downloads, a tap on ⌄ folds it into a small pill with the percent; tap the pill to open it again.
- **The card covered the bottom of a phone's screen**: on a phone it now shows at the top, like a notification; on a computer it stays in the bottom corner.
- **Two cards at once**: a second download waits for the one running instead of pushing it away.
- **Android-only settings showed on a computer** (notification access, moving old downloads out of the app's storage). They now show only on Android.

### Computer
- **Settings like a computer's settings app**: the side list picks one section, shown open on its own. Each setting is one line, its name at the start and its control at the end; choices drop down under themselves. Search still looks through every section, and the section you were on stays in the address.
- **One Player section**: the player's own preferences are now inside «Player» instead of a second «Player settings».
