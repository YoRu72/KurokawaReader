### Fixes
- **Player — sound with a black picture (MKV and anything else mpv plays)**: when the player closed, mpv's video output was switched off with the picture's surface, and the next file was loaded without switching it back on. The first video after starting the app showed its picture; every one after it — the next episode, another file, the same file reopened — played its sound over black. The output is now switched back on whenever a new surface arrives.
- **Player — the ambient glow over mpv**: the glow fills the stage with a tinted layer that covered mpv's picture (which sits under the page); it is left out while mpv plays.
