
## Fixed

- **Furigana and pinyin could not be switched on after a first failed try.** The Reader fetched the reading dictionary only at the moment a book was opened, in thirteen separate pieces; if one failed (the book was first opened without a connection, or the link dropped), it showed "connect to the internet and reopen the book" and never tried again, and there was no button anywhere to get it. On phones nothing was kept either, so every book fetched it all again.
- The notice now has a **Download** button with the real size, shows progress with **Cancel**, and the readings appear in the open book without reopening it. A failure says why (no connection, the server's answer, a damaged file, no space) and offers **Try again**.
- The dictionary is kept on the device once downloaded; books then show readings with no connection. A stopped download continues from the files already fetched, and an incomplete dictionary is never used.
- The sizes shown were wrong: the Japanese dictionary is about 17 MB (not 6) and the Chinese one about 0.3 MB (not 2).
- Selecting Japanese or Chinese text with *Show readings* on, and no dictionary yet, now offers the download in place instead of doing nothing.
- The notice is an ordinary one above the reading bar, no longer an error-coloured block over the text; it does not cover an open sheet, and once closed it stays closed for that book until the option is turned on again.

## New

- **Settings → Reader → Reading dictionaries** (also in the book's text options): Japanese · furigana and Chinese · pinyin, each with its state and size, **Download**, **Cancel** and **Remove**.
- If the kept dictionary cannot be loaded, the Reader loads it the previous way when there is a connection, and otherwise says so with **Try again** and **Remove and download again**.

## Not checked on a real phone

Loading the kept Japanese dictionary was tested here with a stand-in for the analyser, not on a device. If readings do not appear after a download, use **Remove and download again** and tell us.
