### Fixes — swiping and the phone's Back
- **The reader now knows where the phone's Back zone is.** Its width is read from Android (it follows your Back-gesture sensitivity). A touch that begins inside it is the phone's: nothing turns, and Back goes through at once — no more half a page turn followed by Back. A touch that begins outside it is a page turn and never counts as Back. With three-button navigation the whole width turns pages.
- **A scroll that drifts sideways no longer turns the chapter.** The direction is decided once, at the start of the movement.
- **A quick short flick turns the chapter**; before, only a long swipe did.
- **No turn** with two fingers down, while text is selected, with a sheet open, or on a table or code block that can still scroll sideways.
- **Chapter by chapter: no separator line and gap above a chapter's title.** They belong to continuous reading only.

### Changed
- **Book formatting is on by default, and hyphenation is part of it.** One switch: justified lines, words broken at line ends and indented paragraphs, as in a printed book. This update turns it on once for everyone; turn it off and it stays off, and your alignment choice applies again. While it is on, the alignment row says so.
- Titles stay centred and are never indented or hyphenated. Japanese, Chinese and Korean text keeps its own paragraph spacing. Paragraphs inside lists and tables are not indented.

### New (phone)
- **Turn pages by swiping** — on by default; can be switched off.
- **Swipe sensitivity** — Low / Normal / High.
- The Performance page shows the phone's Back zone as the reader saw it, and how many touches were left to the phone.

### Fixes — other
- A chapter title that carries furigana is still recognised, centred, and its label can be hidden.
