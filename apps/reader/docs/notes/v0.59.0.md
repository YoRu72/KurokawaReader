### Fixes
- **Translations into Arabic are set in your Arabic font** (Uthman by default, or the one you chose). A Japanese or Chinese book's own font no longer applies to them.
- **Links still work when translated.** A contents page in the book (titles that take you to their chapter) is translated and each title still takes you there.
- **The translator's bar no longer covers the search** or any other panel or sheet.
- **The translator's X no longer throws the translation away.** It now hides the bar, and the translation stays (see below).
- **The jobs chip no longer covers the translator's bar.** It sits above it.
- **The image viewer's buttons fit** (the close button stuck out of its bar).
- **A whole-book translation goes on after you leave the book.** Its progress stays on screen in the jobs chip and in the notification, with Stop. Opened again, the book shows it is still going instead of starting a second one.
- **Adding a name to the glossary only re-translates the paragraphs that hold it**, not the whole book.

### New
- **Hide the translator's bar.** It folds into a small button at the side, and the translation stays. Tap the button to bring the bar back. The bar's first button turns the translation on or off.
- **Save the translation with the book.** Use the Save button on the bar (or the switch in its ⋮ menu). The book then opens translated every time, offline too, from the translations kept on this device. Turn the translation on or off as usual, and the book opens the way you left it. A book with translated chapters shows the bar when opened.
- **Translate again, as you choose.** The ↻ button offers this chapter, some chapters (from–to), or the whole book: redone if it was translated whole, otherwise translated.
- **Titles translated, the original beneath.** Chapter titles in the text and in the contents. You can also have them translated only, or left as they are (Translator settings → Titles).
- **Characters and places in the glossary on their own** (off until you turn it on in the translator's ⋮ menu). Before a chapter is translated, its names go into the book's glossary, so they are rendered the same everywhere.
- **A better glossary:**
  - Names sorted into Characters, Places, Groups and other terms.
  - This book's or every book's, with a search.
  - Each one changed where it stands.
  - + adds a new line to fill in.
- **Search keeps your last search** (switch in the search). Opened again, it shows the same results instead of an empty box.
- **Step through a PDF's search results with ↑ ↓** in a bar over the page, without opening the search each time.
