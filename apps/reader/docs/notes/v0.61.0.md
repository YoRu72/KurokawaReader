### Fixes
- **No longer thrown out of a book.** A link inside a web novel's chapter, a Markdown or an HTML book (a translator's footnote mark, most often) closed the book when touched — also by a touch meant to turn the page. Such a link now goes to its place in the book, or does nothing.
- **Back in your book after a restart you did not ask for.** When the phone ends the app while you read, the book reopens at your place on the next start. On Android, a failure of the page's engine restarts the reader in place of closing the app, and changing the system font size or screen zoom no longer restarts it.
- **Text no longer sits under the toolbar or the reading bar**, and showing or hiding the bars no longer moves the text. PDF pages end clear of the page bar at the foot or the top, without the jump.
- **Long PDFs no longer take more and more memory as you scroll through them.**
- In Arabic the reading bar's numbers, percentage and clock are written the Arabic way («٤:٤٤ م», «١٥٪»).
- On a book without a cover picture, the «EPUB» and «New» marks no longer cover the title.
- Reading statistics saved in an older shape no longer stop the reading tracker.
- The volume-buttons setting took the buttons everywhere in the app and only scrolled; see below for what it does now.

### Faster
- **EPUBs open at once**: the chapter you are in shows first and the rest of the book is made right after (a 320-chapter book on a slowed phone imitation: 2.5 s → 0.4 s; reopening at your place 2.3 s → 0.2 s).
- **Long text books open about five to eight times faster** and take far less memory.
- **Long PDFs** no longer read every page when opened; reading a PDF's text for search in the background carries on where it stopped, and waits while you scroll.
- **Sync, automatic backup and chapter downloads wait while you scroll or turn pages**, so they no longer cause stalls; one you start yourself runs at once.

### New — reading
- **Volume buttons turn pages** (Android; Reader options → Behaviour; off by default), with **Swap direction**. They work only while a book is open and nothing is playing: during read-aloud or music, and everywhere outside a book, they change the volume as usual. Holding a button keeps turning.
- **Back twice to leave a book** (off by default): the first Back closes what is open, the next shows a reminder, and a second within two seconds leaves. The arrow at the top always leaves at once.
- **Page-turn swipes protected at the screen edges** (Android, on by default, in the chapter modes): a swipe along the middle of the left or right edge changes the chapter in place of being taken as Back. Back still works from the top and bottom of the edge.
- **The reading bar shows what you choose**: page in the chapter «١٢ / ٣٤», pages left in the chapter, the chapter's and the book's percentage, page and pages left in the book, time left, clock, battery, chapter name, book title — each switched on or off, placed at the start, middle or end, in your order (Visual options → Show the reading bar). It stays hidden until you switch it on; "Show / hide the reading bar" is in the More menu and can be a tap-zone action. In immersive mode it has its own switch: keep it while the other bars hide, or hide it with them. For a book of several chapters, "page in the book" is an estimate, marked «≈».
- **Place the reader's buttons as you like** (More → Customise buttons). Every action of the reader, and of the PDF reader, can be put at the start or end of the top bar, on the toolbar, in the More menu, or hidden, and ordered within its place. The toolbar itself can sit at the bottom, the top or either side, floating or docked. What does not fit a narrow screen moves into More by itself. The back arrow, More and "Customise buttons" stay put, so the way out is never lost; a hidden action still answers its shortcut and tap zone. "Reset to default" brings back the usual layout.
- Space and Page Up/Down carry on into the next or previous chapter in "Chapter by chapter" mode too.

### New — settings fit the device
- Options that only concern a phone (tap zones, screen orientation, edge gestures, volume buttons) no longer show on a computer, and ones that only concern a computer (keyboard shortcuts, interface size) no longer show on a phone. A value set on one device and synced to the other is kept, and does nothing where it does not apply.

### New — Performance page
- Settings → About → Performance. The app keeps a small record on your device of start-up time, how long books take to open, slow moments, how each session ended and why a book was left — times, counts and kinds only: no titles, file names or text, and nothing is sent anywhere. "Copy report" gives it as text you can send when something is slow or a book closes by itself.
