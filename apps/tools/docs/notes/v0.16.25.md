### Fixes
- **Swiping a row now works however you hold the phone.** Before, the browser could take a sideways swipe on a row for itself, and the swipe did nothing.

### Gestures
- **Swipe between places**: swipe a page sideways to go to the next or previous place on the bottom bar. The page follows your finger, and the new place slides in from that side. It doesn't get in the way of carousels, chip rows, swipeable cards, fields or the Files drawer, which still opens from its side. Turn it off in Settings → General → "Swipe between places".
- **Drag a sheet down to close it**: any bottom sheet (Add download, menus, options) follows your finger. It closes when dragged a third of the way or flicked, and springs back otherwise. A sheet's list scrolls as before until it reaches its top.
- **Pull to refresh** in Files (the folder is read again, so changes made by other apps show) and in Anime (the open tab is reloaded).
- **Files**: swipe a row back to delete it. You're asked first, as always; the other direction still opens the drawer.
- **Photo and video viewer**: swipe down to close; double tap zooms in on the spot you tapped and back out.
- **On a computer**: the mouse's forward button and Alt+→ go forward, next to the back button and Alt+← that already worked.
