### Fixes
- **Search in book: typing after reopening search.** When search opened again with your last search in it, the cursor stood at the start, so what you typed went in front of the old words. It now stands at the end. This applies to PDFs and to text books.

### Copy buttons
- **AI instructions**: a copy button under the instructions box in AI settings. It copies what is in the box, or the default instructions when the box is empty.
- **Glossary**: a copy button on each entry, and one for the whole glossary. A **paste** button beside it adds entries from copied text (one per line, `source = target`), skipping the ones already there, so a glossary can be moved between books or devices.
- **Notes**: a copy button on each note (the whole quote, your note, the date, and the page for PDFs), and "Copy all" for a book's notes in the order shown, following the All / Bookmarks / Notes filter.
