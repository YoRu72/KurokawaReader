### Fixes
- **«Get a new link by itself» no longer stops on the first page.** The replay opened the page you started from and then did nothing. It now gets there:
  - Taps are recorded as soon as a page shows, not only once it has finished loading. Download sites that keep loading ads were tapped through unrecorded, so their steps were missing.
  - When a page's address differs from the one recorded (the site moved you, say from `example.com` to `www.example.com`, or the page has a new key each visit), the next buttons on the way are looked for on it anyway, in order.
  - A page that never finishes loading still has its step replayed once it is mostly there.
  - A step is never tapped twice.
  - If no step is left to take on a page, a message says so, so you can go on by hand.
  - The way kept is now up to 30 taps long (it was 12).
