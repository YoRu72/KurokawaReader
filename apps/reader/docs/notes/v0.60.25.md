### Fixes — reading position and highlights
- **Highlights return to where they were made.** A highlight on words that occur more than once in a chapter came back on their first occurrence. Each new highlight now remembers its place. (Highlights made before this version have no place recorded and still go to the first occurrence.)
- **Highlights across two paragraphs or two chapters** no longer vanish when the book is reopened.
- **Typographic punctuation**: turning it on or off no longer loses highlights, and no longer doubles a highlighted paragraph's text.
- **A series or merged books** reopen at the book you were last reading, not at the start of the first.
- **Your place is saved when you leave at once.** Leaving the book or the app within a moment of turning a page or scrolling lost that move; in PDFs also a scroll within the same page. And a PDF closed while still opening no longer resets its place to page 1.
- **Closing a book while it is loading** no longer leaves its keys active elsewhere (space, +, −, 0, /, b acting on the library).
- **Changing font size, line spacing or margins** keeps you at the same paragraph.
- **Clearing a note, or editing a PDF highlight's note,** now reaches your other devices through sync.
