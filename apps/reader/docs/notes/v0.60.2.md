### Fixes
- **PDF — zooming in by itself while scrolling fast**: two quick flicks ending at much the same spot were taken for a double tap, and the page zoomed in mid-scroll. Only taps count now — a finger that barely moved, briefly, on a page that was not moving — so a fast scroll never zooms, and a real double tap still does.
- **AI translator — this chapter and the whole book at once**: when both were going, their progress ran together, and stopping one stopped the other. Each translation is now a job of its own, side by side.

### AI translator
- **«This chapter» joins the other choices** in Translate ahead — this chapter, the next chapters, a range, the whole book — and every one goes on **in the background**, the book read meanwhile, out of the reader too.
- **Each job its own line**: what it is (this chapter's name, a range, the whole book), its own progress and its own Stop, in the sheet and in the notification.
- **Nothing translated twice**: a paragraph one job is translating is left to it by the others (and by the bar's translation of the page in view), and counted done once it is kept.
