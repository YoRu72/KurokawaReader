### Fixes
- **Player — the black picture with sound on MKV files** (Erai-raws and the like): mpv was asked for direct MediaCodec output while drawing through the GPU; it now decodes by MediaCodec through the GPU by default, and «direct to the screen» is a separate choice that switches the renderer with it. An MKV/AVI/TS opened with no mpv pack on the phone offers the pack first instead of playing black.
- **Player — subtitles sat behind the control bar**: they are lifted over the bar by its real height now; the chapters tool sits on the top bar by default. The old layout checkbox that would not take is gone — a groups editor replaces it.
- **DoH — sites whose handshake failed** (AniList's «SSL handshake failure» among them): each site's hello is sent in a ladder of ways (whole, split, fragmented, first bytes alone, a decoy with a short TTL) learnt per host from what the middlebox does; the proxy and the app's own requests use the same ladder. Resolver health scoring, a DNS cache with negative entries and stale answers, per-site fallback, ECH/SNI choices and diagnostics.
- **Memory**: a sheet replaced by another kept its Escape listener (found by the new bug hunt, which opens and leaves every page dozens of times while counting listeners, timers, observers and the DOM — flat now).
- **Size**: R8 (minify + shrink) on the Android build with keep rules for everything reached by name; UTF-8 in the bundles (the Arabic chunk 304 K → 136 K); META-INF duplicates excluded.
- Android compile checks run a full release build, so a keep rule that strips too much is caught before release day.
- Downloads added from the grab sheet: select all / deselect all with counts.
- «Get the latest build»: the newest release fetched straight from GitHub past the cache and skipped versions (in case the automatic check misses one).

### Files — the file manager, whole
- **Archives as folders**: a zip, 7z, rar, tar, cbz, jar or apk opened from Files is a place like any folder — breadcrumbs, sort, search, list or grid, pictures inside shown small; a picture inside opens the viewer swiping through its neighbours, a video or song plays in the app's player straight from the archive (nothing unpacked), the rest is taken out and opened by its kind. «Open as a folder» on any archive-like file.
- **Installed apps as folders** (Solid Explorer's way): every app with its icon, package, version and size; inside, its base APK and split APKs (browsable as archives, installable, shareable), its data, obb and media folders (through the granted Android/data tree or root — the grant asked for when missing). Back up the APK (one .apk, or an .apks bundle with its splits), launch, app info, uninstall.
- **The image viewer takes every kind**: HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, ICO, WBMP, JFIF, APNG besides the usual — what the web view cannot draw is decoded by the phone first; «Copy the text» reads the text in a picture (ML Kit), inside archives too.
- **Recent files at the top of the drawer**; big folders drawn in slices as they are scrolled towards (twenty thousand entries open at once).
- Network storages (FTP/FTPS, SFTP, SMB, WebDAV, Nextcloud) and cloud storages (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive with your own client id); a folder shared on the Wi‑Fi (web page + FTP) with Quick Settings tiles; files locked with a password (AES‑256‑GCM), hidden folders, .nomedia; root/Shizuku access with chmod/chown/symlinks/remount; two panes on wide screens with drag and drop; the storage analyzer, archive properties, checksums and EXIF; the text editor; batch rename; the recycle bin; bookmarks pinned to the home screen; Files settings (views, density, themes incl. black and e‑ink, home folder, confirmations).
- Archives engine (HatchZip, whole): split volumes read and extracted in place, bzip2/Deflate64/LZMA/XZ zip entries, 7z options, AES with hidden names, charsets, comments, overlapping and hard‑linked archives refused in place.

### Downloads — the OmniGet and Prism batches (forty‑five items)
- **Engine**: a global connection budget shared by running downloads, per‑host limits that grow back (AIMD), a pause between requests per host, a 403 ladder (Range, fetch hints, Referer, IPv4), media‑signature sniffing and Content‑Length guards, a HEAD probe that routes unknown links, downloads cut short by the app dying marked and offered back, an unreadable task list quarantined, settings back‑compat tests.
- **Speed limits for file downloads**: the global limit and each download's own (a KB/s field on its page), live. Retry‑After honoured; a Cloudflare challenge told apart at once.
- **HLS streams fetched by the app itself** (Android): the best variant at or under the wanted height, pieces fetched several at once and resumable, AES‑128 decrypted, pieces disguised as pictures uncovered, joined and remuxed to MP4 by the ffmpeg pack. «Stream by manifest» in the + menu for an .m3u8/.mpd with the headers a site wants.
- **Video (yt‑dlp)**: a ladder of fixes a refusal climbs by itself; the exact command per attempt with «Edit & retry» and saved argument templates; a live line (resolution · codecs · container · size, fragment i/n, the phase, a stalled mark); quick presets Fast / Quality / Compact / Music (the last remembered); several time ranges, chapters by name, one file a chapter; SponsorBlock as cut‑out or chapter marks with categories; which subtitle languages a page has (automatic captions told apart) and a bilingual .srt merged from two; media‑changed detection between looks; likes and dislikes on YouTube videos; comments, chapters and a live stream's chat beside the file; lyrics (.lrc) beside music.
- **Rules and clocks**: rules by site, word, kind and size that choose folder, quality, subtitles, connections, paused/Wi‑Fi — or capture what the app's browser sees on sight; a start and stop time per download (in an hour, tonight, tomorrow morning, or typed).
- **Batch pre‑flight**: two or more links looked at first — a file with its size, a video page, a torrent, not supported, needs a sign‑in, not found, a web page, already here — the sizes against the free space, and only the ticked ones added.
- **Torrents**: «Ready to play» when a video's head and tail are here, DHT fallback when trackers give no peer, trackers added to one torrent.
- **Cookie jars** (Android): signed‑in states kept encrypted — from the app's browser or a cookies.txt — named, dated, tested, chosen per download.
- **Sidecars for media servers**: a Kodi/Jellyfin/Emby .nfo beside finished videos (episode or movie, with AniList/IMDb ids), the cover kept as a .jpg.
- **Followed channels and playlists** (Videos → Followed): looked at every 1–24 hours; new videos added on their own or counted; a download archive keeps a video from coming twice.
- **Courses** — a new add‑on: a course you are signed in to (Udemy and the other platforms yt‑dlp reads), its outline by section, the ticked lectures downloaded numbered in order into a folder a course. DRM‑protected lectures fail with a plain word; nothing goes around protection.
- **Telegram** public posts and channels (photos and videos, no account); a **profile archiver** for Bluesky profiles, Reddit users and subreddits, Telegram channels; **picture sites**: Danbooru, Safebooru, Gelbooru, Konachan, yande.re on every platform, and on a computer every site gallery‑dl knows.
- **The browser's catch** probed as it comes (type and size; an HLS playlist's size estimated) with a size filter in the grabber.
- **Identical files kept once** (Settings → Downloads): finished files hashed, a second copy of the same bytes becomes a hard link; a storage panel in the statistics.
- **A flight recorder** (Settings → About): the last four hundred events with links cut to their sites and secrets blanked, for bug reports.
- The local stream server's file URLs are signed and expire, so nothing else on the phone can read files through it.
- **A computer**: an icon in the tray with the speed, a system notice when a download finishes, clipboard hotkeys by mode (video / audio / file / the add sheet), managed yt‑dlp with a version floor, the last three kept for a rollback, or your own binary; hardware encoders (NVENC, VideoToolbox, QSV, AMF) for conversions; a local API on 127.0.0.1 behind a token for scripts and browser extensions.
