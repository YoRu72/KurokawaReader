### Fixes
- **Crop margins no longer overloads the phone.** On a long PDF, cropping drew every page of the book in the background and re-laid out the whole book dozens of times while you read, which could freeze the device. It now measures a small sample once, then only the pages near where you are reading, and only while you are not scrolling or zooming.
- **Crop margins: nothing is cut off.** Light text, small page numbers, thin lines, tinted paper and dark pages are now recognised. A page that can't be measured is shown whole.
- **Crop margins: pages stay put.** Turning cropping on or off keeps your place, pages no longer jump or flicker as you read, and all ordinary pages have the same width. Title pages and full-page pictures are fitted to that width.
- **Pinch zoom holds its point.** The page no longer slides off your fingers and jumps back when you let go (up to 36 px at full zoom), with cropping on as well.
- Letting go of a pinch below the screen width now glides back into place instead of jumping sideways.
- A two-finger pan without zooming no longer springs back.
- A pinch started while a zoom button's animation was running could hang and leave the page stretched.
- A double tap straight after a pinch is no longer missed.
- Pinching is lighter on the phone (less work for every finger movement).

Note: a page you jump straight to (from search or the slider) is measured as soon as scrolling stops. If it is an unusual page, its outer edge may show a moment later.
